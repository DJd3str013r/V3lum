- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=noncommercial, opsec=passive, shares_input=True

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Two modules. ct_logs (default) queries crt.sh, Cert Spotter and ctl.shodan.io. ct_logs_extra (default_enabled=False) holds crt.name and MerkleMap, whose operators are unknown tier (A8) until a promotion is recorded: excluded at AMBER and above, --allow-unknown at CLEAR/GREEN.
2. Per-source terms in the registry: crt.sh open (Sectigo, trusted); Cert Spotter keyed = key-terms, keyless = noncommercial (SSLMate: 'personal or evaluation'); ctl.shodan.io = noncommercial, same licence decision as Shodan InternetDB; crt.name unknown; MerkleMap key-terms on a company account.
3. ctl.shodan.io and keyless Cert Spotter are off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared (licence_allow entry or Shodan licence); Cert Spotter runs with the team's own key once key_plans.certspotter is recorded (A1). crt.sh alone keeps the module useful. Keys certspotter, crtname, merklemap go in config.KNOWN_KEYS, Bearer header only.
4. crt.sh: GET https://crt.sh/?q=%25.{domain}&output=json&exclude=expired. Sources run concurrently inside one module budget (default 30 s, setting max 90 s; retries on 502/503/timeout bounded by it, AR05). A crt.sh timeout/5xx with no other answer = could not verify, never 'no certificates'. Per-source status is kept.
5. A body truncated at the core 15 MB cap is never parsed as complete: mark partial=True and use Cert Spotter (after=<last id> pagination, at most 5 pages). Declared max_requests <= 10 per entity, expected_latency <= 10 s.
6. One shared core hostname-sink helper (also used by P007/P016): split name_value on newlines, lowercase, strip '*.', Entity.try_make + PSL/IANA TLD check, keep names ending in .{apex}, dedupe, provenance list per name. Wildcard entries become a wildcard_cert attribute of the parent, not names.
7. Run once per registrable domain: subdomain entities reuse the apex result and trigger no CT request. Subdomain pivots are bounded by max_pivots/EXPAND_CAP; cert sha256 becomes cert_fingerprint pivots with expand=False. CT-only names that get NXDOMAIN through DoH are labelled historical and get passive modules only; a DoH error or timeout leaves them unresolved (could not verify, rule 5).
8. Out-of-apex SAN names become cert_san affiliate pivots (expand=False). Shared certificates (> 20 SANs over > 5 registrable domains, or CDN/hosting shared-cert issuer/subject patterns, P034 ranges) give only a shared_cert attribute: no affiliate pivots, excluded from CR31 and seeds_linked.
9. Earliest not_before is reported as first_cert_seen (a lower bound of age), never as a registration date. The report shows counts (certs, unique names, earliest not_before, issuers) and the top 20 names; the full list goes to the JSON/HTML appendix.
10. Brand mode is opt-in (setting ct_brand_search, default false), because the keyword query discloses the brand to crt.sh (as for DomainsDB, P005). When on and the seed is an org_profile domain: one crt.sh identity search q=%25{brand}%25 per scan for org_profile brands of >= 4 chars; out-of-profile names become lookalike pivots (expand=False) for P028.
11. Tests with trimmed fixtures (crt.sh JSON, Cert Spotter page, crt.name text, ctl.shodan.io JSON): multi-line name_value split, '*.' stripping, out-of-apex names dropped, truncated body, crt.sh timeout still yields Cert Spotter names with partial status, a subdomain triggers no CT request, a multi-tenant certificate links nothing.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1. All six members asked to keep the small operators out of the default set (crt.name, MerkleMap: cti_lead, osint_investigator, redteam_lead, legal_dpo). The chair puts them in a separate opt-in module so the default module holds only trusted providers (A3 BR2, A8 BR12). ## P002 `email_security_posture` - E-mail security posture (SPF tree, DMARC, DKIM selectors, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, BIMI, CAA, DNSSEC)

- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: cloudflare-dns.com, dns.google

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. DNS only through ctx.dns (core DoH): SPF, DMARC (p, pct, sp, rua, ruf), DKIM over a bundled common-selector list capped at 30, MTA-STS TXT, TLS-RPT, BIMI, CAA and the DNSSEC AD flag. No SMTP/STARTTLS connection to MX hosts (checkdmarc's SMTP checks are not ported). The MTA-STS policy file fetch belongs to wellknown_files (P065, LIGHT), which owns the single MTA-STS parser.
2. SPF evaluator bounded by RFC 7208: 10 DNS-mechanism lookups, 2 void lookups, loop detection on include/redirect, recursion depth <= 10 and a hard cap of 40 DoH queries per run. It outputs spf_lookups and spf_void_lookups for P027 CR02.
3. DMARC discovery: for a subdomain query _dmarc.<name>, then the organisational domain (PSL) and apply its sp= tag. Check the external-report authorization record (<domain>._report._dmarc.<dest>) for at most 5 rua/ruf destinations and output dmarc_rua_external.
4. Rule 5: a DoH error, SERVFAIL, REFUSED or timeout on TXT, _dmarc or MX means could not verify. No 'missing SPF/DMARC' finding is raised on missing data; a dead-DoH unit test proves it (email_basics has this defect today).
5. SPF mining: CIDRs reached through include:/a:/mx: of SaaS/ESP providers (Google, Microsoft, SendGrid, Mailchimp, Amazon SES... per the noise list) are tagged third_party_mail, expand=False, never treated as the organisation's netblocks, never fed to reputation/attack-surface rules and never eligible for an ACTIVE scope.
6. Only direct ip4/ip6 mechanisms of the domain's own record become candidate CIDR pivots (expand=False) until RDAP/NIC.br confirms ownership.
7. Severity by ownership: for org_profile own domains, missing DMARC or p=none -> MEDIUM, SPF +all -> HIGH, SPF permerror (> 10 lookups) -> MEDIUM. Any other domain gets ONE info 'posture summary' finding (SPF/DMARC/DKIM/MTA-STS/BIMI table); P027 CR01 raises it when the case warrants. Absent BIMI/MTA-STS/TLS-RPT is never above info.
8. DKIM: no key under the common selectors is worded 'not determined (N common selectors checked)', never 'DKIM missing'. Runs on registrable domains and e-mail domains only, once per scan.
9. checkdmarc logic is re-implemented (Apache-2.0, attribution in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) without importing dnspython; recon-ng (GPL) is idea only. New attribute names (spf_lookups, spf_void_lookups, dmarc_pct, dmarc_sp, dmarc_rua_external, dkim_selectors, mta_sts, tls_rpt, bimi) are added to core/vocab.py in the same change.
10. Tests with trimmed DoH fixtures: an include loop, a > 10-lookup SPF tree, a dead DoH answer that must not yield a 'missing' finding, a third-party domain without DMARC giving one info finding, an own domain giving the MEDIUM finding.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1. The architect's floor moves the MTA-STS HTTPS fetch out of this passive module into P065 and bounds the SPF evaluator (40 queries, 30 selectors; redteam_lead's 60-selector cap is looser). cti_lead and redteam_lead require the third-party tag on SaaS netblocks so Microsoft/Google ranges never reach scans or scopes. cti_lead and analyst_ux require ownership-based severity, with ownership taken from org_profile as in P027. legal_dpo asked only for the checkdmarc attribution. Data travels through the core DoH resolvers (Google/Cloudflare): tier trusted, terms open (P029).
