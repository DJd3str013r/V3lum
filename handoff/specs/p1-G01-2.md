## P012 `typosquat` - Typosquat / brand-impersonation engine (dnstwist algorithms + DoH registration check)

- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=community, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: domain_dns; group: G01; inputs: domain, keyword; produces: domain
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: dnstwister.report

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Permutation engine as one shared core helper (also used by nrd_feeds P005 and watch P030), re-implementing the dnstwist fuzzers with the Apache-2.0 NOTICE: addition, bitsquatting, homoglyph, hyphenation, insertion, omission, repetition, replacement, subdomain, transposition, vowel-swap, dictionary and TLD swap. IDN candidates are validated with IDNA/UTS-46 before any query.
2. BR set: the pt-BR lure dictionary (>= 60 words: pix, boleto, fatura, 2via, atualizar, cadastro, seguranca, desbloqueio, token, resgate, pontos, beneficio, fgts, inss, cnh, detran, ipva, receita, cpf, gov, app, online...) lives in data/brand_lures_pt.json (shared with P028), own work or permissively licensed, never GPL/CC BY-SA.
3. Combo-squats {brand}-{word}, {word}-{brand}, {brand}{word}; accent homoglyphs (a/á/â/ã, e/é/ê, i/í, o/ó/ô/õ, u/ú, c/ç) plus rn/m, vv/w, l/1, o/0; TLD swaps incl. com.br, net.br, app.br, online, site, top, xyz, shop, vip. Unit test: brand 'itau' yields itau-seguranca.com.br and the punycode of itaú.com.br.
4. Reverse mode (default, offline, < 1 s): is the INPUT a lookalike of a protected domain (org_profile brands/domains, BCB DominiosVerificados P360, gov.br list P009)? If so: a lookalike pivot and a finding. Forward scans run only on analyst-typed org_profile domains/brands or with explicit -M typosquat, never on pivots, subdomains or arbitrary hostile domains (run_on='seeds').
5. Forward caps: 150 top-ranked candidates in a default scan (500 with --preset deep or watch); staged checks, NS/A first and MX/AAAA only for registered names; DoH budget 300 queries per run (configurable), <= 10 q/s, 45 s module budget, ctx.cancelled() checks; a hit cap is reported in coverage.
6. Registration is checked only through the core DoH resolver with ECS disabled (P029), never the system/corporate resolver, and never by registro.br availability calls (P021). RDAP for registered candidates goes through the P003 helper, at most 10 lookups per run (20 with deep), and lookalike registrant data follows the P003 minimisation.
7. Rule 5: a DoH SERVFAIL, REFUSED or timeout on a candidate means 'could not verify' (counted in coverage), never 'not registered'; only an NXDOMAIN answer for the queried types counts as unregistered.
8. Severity ladder (unit-tested): registered only = INFO, counted in one info table; one activity signal (MX, CT certificate <= 30 d, NRD <= 30 d, non-parking A record) = MEDIUM; HIGH needs >= 2 weaponisation signals (also phishing-list hit, LIGHT page similarity) AND evidence it is not brand-owned. This applies to BCB/gov.br protected domains too.
9. Not brand-owned means: not an org_profile domain, RDAP registrant/CNPJ root differs from org_profile (or from the institution's CNPJ in the BCB list), NS/MX not the brand's, registrar not a brand-protection registrar (MarkMonitor, CSC, Com Laude...). Own defensive registrations are listed in the own-asset section, never as typosquat findings. Parking-NS/sinkhole lists cap lookalikes at LOW.
10. No LIGHT contact in this module: candidates are hostile by default, and HTTP, page-similarity and MX checks live in typosquat_live_checks behind the P376 guard. Lookalike pivots are expand=False. The terminal shows the top 10 by score; the rest goes to HTML/JSON. Lookalikes of org_profile brands feed watch mode (P030).
11. dnstwister.report (discloses the brand) is a separate opt-in adapter module typosquat_dnstwister: default_enabled=False, community tier, excluded at AMBER and above, one fuzz call per brand.
12. Tests: reverse match, defensive registration, the 'itau' combo set, parked cap, cap hit, ECS present on every DoH query.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1. cti_lead, osint_investigator and analyst_ux supplied the severity ladder and own-asset suppression, reconciled with P028 (cti_lead's two-signal HIGH, osint_investigator's MEDIUM for one signal, BCB banks not HIGH by themselves). redteam_lead: DoH without ECS only and no LIGHT contact. legal_dpo's floor: Apache NOTICE, no copyleft word lists, dnstwister opt-in and excluded at AMBER+, P003 minimisation, no registro.br availability calls. The architect's floor: one shared helper, IDNA validation, staged checks with a hard DoH budget, seeds only, expand=False pivots. analyst_ux: the reverse mode and the 150-candidate interactive cap. The BCB and gov.br lists stay protected domains as the approved proposal says. Tier community because RDAP fallback goes through rdap.org; terms open (own code over DoH/RDAP).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Run permutations locally (dnstwist fuzzers re-implemented with Apache-2.0 NOTICE: addition, bitsquatting, homoglyph, hyphenation, insertion, omission, repetition, replacement, subdomain, transposition, vowel-swap, dictionary incl. a pt-BR word list, TLD swap with abused-TLD list); check registration through DoH (A/AAAA/MX/NS) and RDAP for registered ones; cap candidates (default 500) and DoH rate. dnstwister.report API only as optional fallback: it discloses the brand to a third party (off by default). Live checks of lookalikes (MX-spy, page similarity) live in typosquat_live_checks. Gap fill: protected brands include the BCB verified financial-institution domains (bcb_authorized_institutions, 532 domains) and gov.br official domains; a registered look-alike of a verified bank domain is HIGH.
Notes: Core brand-protection capability; drives CR04-CR06 and CR35 with NRD feeds and CT logs.

## P059 `web_archives` - Web archives (Wayback CDX + availability, Arquivo.pt, Common Crawl, CarbonDate, UKWA)

- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: url_web; group: G01; inputs: domain, url; produces: url, domain
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: web.archive.org, archive.org, arquivo.pt, index.commoncrawl.org, carbondate.cs.odu.edu, webarchive.org.uk
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0144": ["P287"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Default scan: the Wayback availability API (archive.org/wayback/available) plus one CDX summary query per registrable seed domain/URL inside a 15 s budget. CDX uses collapse=urlkey, fl=original,timestamp,statuscode,mimetype, limit/page parameters and a total row cap of 20,000; a body truncated at the core cap is partial, never complete. Gentle use (~15 req/min).
2. Arquivo.pt CDX (NDJSON) is queried for .br/.pt and Portuguese-language sites even when Wayback answers, and as the fallback when web.archive.org is unreachable.
3. Common Crawl (latest 1-2 indexes only, backoff; records via Range from data.commoncrawl.org) and CarbonDate run only with --preset deep or -M. CarbonDate may read the live URI: its source is flagged fetches_target and never receives URLs carrying tokens or recipient identifiers. UKWA stays disabled until it answers.
4. Per-source status. Connection resets, 504 and timeouts give could-not-verify ('archive unreachable'), never 'never archived' or 'no captures'.
5. Before pivots, attributes, res.raw, evidence and reports, the core URL-redaction helper masks userinfo (user:pass@), tokens, session ids, query-string secrets (P312 detectors) and e-mail addresses in archived URLs; unit test with the user:pass@ sample.
6. Archived bodies are fetched only from archive hosts (id_ raw snapshots), never via the live site, handled as untrusted data (never executed or rendered), and kept only as extracted indicators plus SHA-256, not stored in full.
7. Archived URLs are never requested from the live target in any mode, except by the ACTIVE path module within an authorized scope (replaying archived tokens is credential use). Save Page Now and any 'save'/'archive now' endpoint are never called (rule 7, P080).
8. Outputs: archive_first (a lower bound of web presence, never a registration date; no captures never means a new domain), archive_last, archive_count and the closest snapshot link; archived_url pivots expand=False and capped; URL lists go to JSON/appendix (P060). first_seen = min(RDAP created, first CT not_before, first capture).
9. Archive-first: web_archives exposes the closest archived homepage snapshot (id_ body from the archive host, 1 MB cap, within the 15 s budget) so modules needing homepage-derived data (title, CNPJ text, analytics ids, favicon, contacts, P072 matchers) use it in passive mode.
10. Registry terms (A1 BR3): the Internet Archive (Wayback CDX and availability), Arquivo.pt (FCT public service), Common Crawl (free terms of use) and CarbonDate (ODU academic service) are 'open', each recorded by legal with evidence; the Internet Archive grants access for research, so legal records internal CTI research as the purpose. UKWA stays disabled. Volume stays low (rule 6).
11. Tests with the recorded availability, Arquivo.pt CDX, CarbonDate and UKWA 403 fixtures plus a truncated CDX body and a connection reset.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1 (cti_lead voted P2). legal_dpo's floor: the research-purpose record for the Internet Archive, masking of credentials and victims' e-mails before evidence, bodies kept only as indicators, no 'save' endpoint. The architect's floor: CDX caps, per-source status, the core redaction helper, capped expand=False pivots. redteam_lead: no replay of archived URLs against the target and CarbonDate flagged fetches_target. osint_investigator: Arquivo.pt for Portuguese sites and archive-first homepage data. analyst_ux: the 15 s default budget, deep-only slow sources and the combined first_seen. All archives are established non-profits or public bodies: tier trusted, terms open.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Wayback CDX (collapse=urlkey, fl=original,timestamp,statuscode,mimetype, paginate) with long timeout and retries: web.archive.org reset connections from datacenter egress (4/4) while archive.org/wayback/available worked -> availability API as fallback, then Arquivo.pt CDX (verified), Common Crawl latest 1-2 indexes only (index server 504/resets when overloaded), CarbonDate for first-seen estimates. UK Web Archive CDX returned 403 'maintenance': keep disabled until it answers. Mask credentials embedded in archived URLs (user:pass@, tokens) before storing. Results: archive_first, archive_count, archived_url pivots (expand=False).
Notes: URL history, first-seen dates and deleted content; base for archive_mining techniques.
