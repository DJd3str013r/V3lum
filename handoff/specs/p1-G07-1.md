## P153 `otx` - AlienVault/LevelBlue OTX indicators (general, reputation, passive DNS, URL list, malware)

- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=key-terms, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: threat_intel_ioc; group: G07; inputs: ipv4, ipv6, domain, url, md5, sha1, sha256, cve; produces: domain, ipv4, url, malware_family, threat_actor
- auth: none; key env vars: V3LUM_OTX_KEY; hosts: otx.alienvault.com

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Two module classes sharing one parser: otx (requires key 'otx', header X-OTX-API-KEY, default_enabled True, NEEDS_KEY without a key) and otx_keyless (no key, 'general' section only, default_enabled False, because keyless calls take ~25 s and hit 429 on shared egress: A3 latency rule).
2. Endpoints: otx.alienvault.com/api/v1/indicators/{IPv4|IPv6|domain|hostname|url|file|cve}/{value}/{section}: 'general' for all types; passive_dns only for seeds; url_list and malware only when pulse_count > 0 or in --preset deep. Read-only: never create pulses or submit indicators.
3. Terms: the OTX terms page is JS-only and was not verified (2026-11-26): legal records the OTX terms for a company account; source class 'key-terms', so under corporate it runs only with free_plan_business_use=true or key_plans.otx='business'. Registry provider otx (LevelBlue): tier trusted.
4. Bounds: per-call timeout 20 s in the default preset and 60 s in --preset deep, always within the scan deadline (timeout = could not verify); max_bytes 5 MB (truncated = partial, never parsed as complete); pulses capped at 50 by recency; cache 24 h. 403 with a key = NEEDS_KEY (bad key); 429 = RATE_LIMITED, never 'not listed'.
5. Pulse membership is not a verdict: validation[]/whitelist entries and warninglist hits give info/noise and no malicious finding. Tag malicious only with >= 2 pulses from distinct authors modified in the last 365 days or 1 pulse by the AlienVault/LevelBlue labs account; otherwise 'mentioned in N community pulses' (low).
6. Findings show the pulse count and the top 3 pulse names/authors/dates at low-medium confidence; pulse dates feed observed_first/observed_last (STIX valid_from). Pulses relaying abuse.ch count as the abuse.ch upstream family (P171).
7. adversary, malware_families and attack_ids -> attributed_to/family/uses_technique pivots with expand=False, normalised through the actor alias index (P295). E-mail and username indicators taken from community pulses are pivots with expand=False.
8. Fixtures: general for 8.8.8.8 (whitelisted), a domain with pulses, keyless passive_dns 429 and a bad-key 403.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0. The tally median is P2 on a 3-3 tie (cti_lead, the architect and analyst_ux P1; the others P2); the chair raises it to P1 because, with abuse.ch MalwareBazaar/URLhaus/ThreatFox licence-gated and VirusTotal public non-commercial, OTX with the recommended free key (A15) is one of the few sources that can meet the A3 minimum answer set (2 default-ready reputation sources per type under corporate). legal_dpo's floor makes the terms 'key-terms' with a company account (stricter than the draft's 'open'). The architect's key-only default (keyless is opt-in for latency), cti_lead's pulse thresholds and analyst_ux's bounded sections are consolidated.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): indicators/{IPv4|IPv6|domain|hostname|url|file|cve}/{value}/{general|reputation|geo|malware|url_list|passive_dns}; keyless works but is slow (~25 s for domain/general) and rate-limited (429 on pdns from shared egress) -> 60 s timeout, cache 24 h, optional V3LUM_OTX_KEY (X-OTX-API-KEY; a bad key returns 403); pulse names/tags/adversary -> attributes and attributed_to/family pivots (low confidence for community pulses).
Notes: Broad community TI with pulses linking IOCs to campaigns/actors.

- category: threat_intel_ioc; group: G07; inputs: url, domain, ipv4, md5, sha1, sha256, malware_family; produces: url, domain, ipv4, sha256, malware_family
- auth: free_key; key env vars: V3LUM_ABUSECH_KEY, V3LUM_ABUSECH_AUTH_KEY; hosts: threatfox-api.abuse.ch, urlhaus-api.abuse.ch, mb-api.abuse.ch, urlhaus.abuse.ch, threatfox.abuse.ch, bazaar.abuse.ch, feodotracker.abuse.ch, sslbl.abuse.ch
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0408": ["P351"], "C0332": ["P350"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Module threat_intel_ioc/abusech covers the URLhaus, ThreatFox and MalwareBazaar query APIs and their v2 exports, plus Feodo Tracker and SSLBL. One key 'abusech' (exists; V3LUM_ABUSECH_KEY and V3LUM_ABUSECH_AUTH_KEY only as aliases) comes from a company account, is sent as header Auth-Key on every call (terms s.2.1) and is shared with yaraify, offline_hash_sets and structural_hash_pivots.
2. Licence per source (A1 BR14): MalwareBazaar, URLhaus and ThreatFox are 'license' (terms 2026-10-21 s.4.1): off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared (a Spamhaus subscription or a written abuse.ch/Spamhaus decision in licence_allow). Feodo Tracker and SSLBL are 'open' (CC0 incl. commercial use, s.3.4) and run through ctx.allowed_sources.
3. Lands only after AR01 redaction: v2 export URLs embed the key in the path (https://{urlhaus|threatfox|mb}-api.abuse.ch/v2/files/exports/{AUTH_KEY}/...) and must never reach the cache url column, events, errors, evidence or reports; the dataset registry stores an {AUTH_KEY} template.
4. Read-only by construction: a per-API query allow-list with a test proves that get_file, sample downloads, uploads, ThreatFox submit_ioc and URLhaus submissions are never built (rule 7).
5. query_status is mapped centrally: ok; no_result(s) -> EMPTY; illegal_* -> SKIPPED reason_code 'not_applicable' (input error shown once); anything else -> ERROR. 401 -> NEEDS_KEY. The legacy keyless exports (410 Gone) are never used.
6. Bulk: pasted lists of > 20 IOCs are matched against local exports, not per-IOC API calls (s.7.3 forbids high-volume scripted harvesting). Exports go through the dataset manager: recent.csv by default, refreshed at most hourly; full.csv.zip only via `v3lum data update --full` with safe_extract caps; one local SQLite index.
7. Severity: URLhaus online or last_online <= 30 days -> high; offline > 90 days -> low 'historical'; ThreatFox confidence_level < 50 -> suspicious and feeds the P322 label. A URLhaus host that is a popular file-hosting/CDN domain (P169 warninglists) -> info 'abused legitimate service', never malicious.
8. All abuse.ch feeds are one upstream family for consensus (P171). Malpedia-style family ids are normalised through the alias index (P295). Returned URLs, domains and IPs are hostile-tagged for the P376 guard. Feodo Tracker and SSLBL are registered once in the dataset manager and shared with ip_blocklists (P149) and ioc_feeds_offline (P159), never downloaded twice.
9. Without the key: one coverage line per scan ('needs abuse.ch Auth-Key, free: v3lum keys setup') plus manual URLhaus/ThreatFox/MalwareBazaar links, not per-entity errors. Findings name platform, status (online/offline), family and first/last seen; abuse.ch attribution is kept in exports.
10. Success fixtures are captured with the team key before merge (only 401/410 bodies exist).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1. Every member confirms the 2026-10-21 terms: corporate use of MalwareBazaar, URLhaus and ThreatFox needs a Spamhaus subscription or a written decision, while Feodo Tracker and SSLBL are CC0 (A1 BR14); A3 BR4 keeps the module a default candidate that is off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared, with the open feeds running meanwhile. The architect's redaction-first sequencing, redteam_lead's query allow-list, cti_lead's freshness rules and bulk-export path, and analyst_ux's single coverage line are consolidated.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): All APIs return 401 without the free Auth-Key and, since 21 Oct 2026, legacy keyless exports return 410 Gone -> one setting V3LUM_ABUSECH_KEY sent as header 'Auth-Key'; POST form APIs with query_status in the JSON body (no_results / illegal_* are not errors); new export pattern https://{urlhaus|threatfox}-api.abuse.ch/v2/files/exports/{AUTH_KEY}/{recent.csv|full.csv.zip} matched locally (never log the key-bearing URL).
