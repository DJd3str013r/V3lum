- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=community, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: brazil_public_data; group: G10; inputs: cnpj; produces: cnpj, company_name, person_name, email, phone, cep, domain
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: brasilapi.com.br, minhareceita.org, api.opencnpj.org, open.cnpja.com, publica.cnpj.ws, receitaws.com.br, api.queridodiario.org.br

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Sources at release (class open, legal_dpo): BrasilAPI and Minha Receita (MIT code, RFB open data; one shared parser), publica.cnpj.ws public API (only free source of state IE) and Querido Diario company/partners. Tiers: BrasilAPI, Minha Receita, CNPJ.ws and Querido Diario (Open Knowledge Brasil) community. Default-on by A3 decision; a CPF is never looked up.
2. OpenCNPJ, CNPJa open and ReceitaWS are coded behind the same normaliser but registered as sources only after legal records their terms (A1: unknown terms run in no profile) and the reviewer records a trusted/community tier (A8: unknown tiers never receive a CNPJ). Until then they are not called and coverage lists them as 'pending terms review'.
3. Chain: stop at the first full record, 8 s per provider, 25 s chain budget, per-provider status in coverage. Per-provider budgets come from the registry (P334): publica.cnpj.ws and ReceitaWS 3/min (honour x-ratelimit-remaining/reset), CNPJa ~5/min; an empty bucket skips to the next provider instead of sleeping (no wait > 2 s); 3/min providers only as last resort.
4. One normaliser to one schema: names, situacao with its date, opening date, CNAEs (labels via P235 once it lands; raw codes until then), legal nature, size, capital, Simples/MEI, address + IBGE code, phones, e-mails, QSA, state IE.
5. Every field keeps its provider and the provider's freshness field (updated, ultima_atualizacao, data_situacao_cadastral); when several providers answered, conflicts are shown with their source and the freshest wins.
6. Not found (rule 5): a valid-DV CNPJ is 'not in RFB open data as of <snapshot date> (companies opened after the last dump may be missing)' only after not_found from two providers with different upstreams; a not_found from a dump-based provider triggers at most one such fallback. One 404 while others error, 429, 5xx or all failing = could not verify.
7. ReceitaWS {'status':'ERROR'} inside HTTP 200 is ERROR (P321 marker). Alphanumeric CNPJs are accepted (providers answer 404 not_found, not 400). Cache TTL 72 h.
8. Registry contacts: RFB e-mail/phone are attributes plus pivots with relation rfb_contact and expand=False, labelled 'registry contact (often the accounting firm)'; never auto-expanded into breach, stealer, username or phone-reputation modules; correlation rules never treat a shared contact as same owner; contacts shared by > 20 CNPJs (via P238 when installed) are tagged shared_contact.
9. Contact domains: free and BR webmail domains (gmail, hotmail, outlook, yahoo, bol, uol, terra, ig...) never become domain pivots; other contact domains pivot with expand=False unless registro.br RDAP ties the domain to the same CNPJ root.
10. QSA (rule 4, legal floor): natural-person partners become person_name pivots (relation partner_of), shown only in the CNPJ card with the RFB-masked CPF digits as given, never unmasked, completed or joined across sources (no rule joins on CPF fragments), age band only in the QSA table, never auto-expanded; only offline sanctions/PEP screening in due-diligence cases.
11. PJ partners are CNPJ pivots and the engine's depth and entity budget decide (no recursion inside the module): default depth 1 for partner companies (configurable to 2), at most 20 companies per seed. Partner and branch CNPJs and their domains are third parties: passive modules only, never LIGHT/ACTIVE seeds, never added to org_profile or a scope automatically.
12. MEI/EI: CPF digits embedded in the razao social are masked at ingestion, before display, evidence and indexes; their e-mails and phones are personal data (domain pivots only for non-webmail domains, no automatic breach/stealer/phone modules). Evidence keeps the response in this masked form under A10 retention.
13. Findings: situacao other than ATIVA (INAPTA, SUSPENSA, BAIXADA, NULA) is a MEDIUM finding with its date and meaning; opening date and company age are attributes for P258 (CR33), with no age-only finding. The card header shows provider used, data date, plain-language status, opening date and age. The legal-name pivot never triggers cnpj_name_resolver (P361).
14. Merge gate (A16): offline tests with FakeHttp (unmatched requests fail, network guard active) over trimmed real fixtures without personal data (the 11 captures plus a 404 and a 429 per provider, the alphanumeric-CNPJ 404 and the ReceitaWS 200-ERROR body), a provider-registry entry for every host, the registry contract test and the P327 lint.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1: the core Brazilian pivot, default-on by A3. legal_dpo's floor decides the sources (terms-recorded providers by default) together with A1/A8: unreviewed providers run in no profile and unknown tiers never receive a CNPJ, so OpenCNPJ, CNPJa and ReceitaWS wait for review, which also keeps Module.terms 'open'. It also sets the CPF/QSA and MEI masking. The architect's floor: registry-driven per-provider budgets that skip instead of sleeping, one normaliser, no recursion inside the module, the two-upstream not-found rule. cti_lead, osint_investigator, redteam_lead and analyst_ux supplied the accountant-contact trap handling, provenance, time budget, third-party rule and card layout. cti_lead's MEDIUM status finding is kept as a fact, and osint_investigator's age-as-attribute rule as well.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Fallback chain with per-provider token buckets and 72 h cache: BrasilAPI -> Minha Receita (same schema, one parser) -> OpenCNPJ -> CNPJa open (~5/min) -> publica.cnpj.ws (3/min, only free source of state IE) -> ReceitaWS (3/min; HTTP 200 with status ERROR = error; may be stale) -> Querido Diario company/info. Normalise to one schema (names, status, dates, CNAEs, legal nature, size, capital, Simples/MEI, address+IBGE, phones, e-mails, QSA). All accept the alphanumeric CNPJ (404 not_found, not 400). QSA partners: names with CPF masked by RFB -> show only in CNPJ context, never unmask/complete CPFs (LGPD); PJ partners -> CNPJ recursion 1-2 levels; e-mail domains -> domain modules.
Notes: Core Brazilian company resolver; feeds QSA/partner graph and due diligence.
