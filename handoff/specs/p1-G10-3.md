### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Datasets (dataset manager only, never during a scan): BCB Olinda DominiosVerificados (~630 KB) downloaded daily in full and matched locally, because server-side $filter/$count are broken; the slow Sedes* sets (3-35 s) and the CVM registries refreshed by `v3lum data update` through one CVM loader shared with P244/P361.
2. Each copy is validated (row count close to the previous one) before replacing it; the dataset date goes into evidence and coverage.
3. OData comment-wrapped error bodies ('/*{...}*/') are ERROR, never an empty list (P321). Default-on by A3 decision (BCB authorised institutions and DominiosVerificados).
4. Matching uses the PSL registrable domain and exact host, or a subdomain of a verified domain; never substrings, lookalikes or string suffix matching. A match sets known_good plus an INFO finding with legal name, CNPJ root, segment and conglomerate.
5. known_good annotates, never suppresses: it lowers domain-reputation weight only, never downgrades malicious findings from other sources (a flagged verified domain surfaces as 'possible compromise - verified institution domain, review URL'), and URL-level hits stay.
6. A URL on a verified domain whose query or fragment carries a redirect target on another host (open-redirect pattern) is not known_good and is tagged 'redirect via verified domain'.
7. Absence produces no finding: at most an INFO line 'no verified domain registered with BCB' (1,066 institutions have none), never a fraud signal.
8. Protected-brand feed for P012/P028: a lookalike of a verified institution domain is HIGH only when registered and active (resolves, MX, fresh certificate or live page), otherwise a MEDIUM watch item. A lookalike whose registro.br registrant CNPJ root equals the institution's is defensive and suppressed; conglomerate registrants may legitimately differ.
9. CNPJ-root or name queries answer in one line 'authorised by BCB/CVM: yes (<segment>) / not found as of <date>'. CVM natural-person registries (advisers, portfolio managers) are matched only in explicit due diligence, with CPF masked and never a lookup key (rule 4).
10. Tiers: BCB and CVM trusted (government). Attribution BCB/CVM in reports; CVM's ODbL share-alike applies only if a derived database is redistributed. Fixtures: trimmed real DominiosVerificados and Sedes rows plus a comment-wrapped error body. BACKLOG: authorised betting operators (.bet.br, SPA/MF) once a data source is verified.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1: bank and fintech impersonation dominates Brazilian phishing and this is the strongest official known-good list. The architect's floor: full daily download through the dataset manager, PSL matching, known_good that annotates and never suppresses. cti_lead and osint_investigator stop known_good at the domain (open redirects, compromised pages) and supply the defensive-registration and active-lookalike rules. legal_dpo's minimisation for natural-person registries and analyst_ux's one-line answer complete it.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Daily download of BCB DominiosVerificados (~630 KB; server-side $filter/$count unreliable) and Sedes* sets (slow: 3-35 s, cache) plus CVM intermediary/adviser/portfolio-manager/crowdfunding registries; exact or parent-domain match -> known_good with legal name/CNPJ root and conglomerate; protected-brand feed for typosquat (P012) and correlation (look-alike of a verified bank domain = HIGH); 'is this bank/fintech/broker/consortium authorised?' by CNPJ root or name; absence from the 532 verified domains is not evidence of fraud (1,066 institutions have none).
Notes: Bank/fintech impersonation dominates Brazilian phishing; this is the strongest official known-good list.
