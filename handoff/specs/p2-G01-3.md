## P016 `subdomains_passive` - Keyless passive subdomain aggregator (THC, AnubisDB, Subdomain Center, subdomain.app, myssl, ScanMalware, RapidDNS)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=community, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: domain_dns; group: G01; inputs: domain; produces: domain
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: ip.thc.org, anubisdb.com, jldc.me, api.subdomain.center, api.subdomain.app, myssl.com, scanmalware.com, rapiddns.io

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. One adapter per source with its own token bucket and per-source status; a blocked source is recorded as BLOCKED, never as zero names, and coverage shows 'k/N sources answered'. Adapters run concurrently inside one 30 s module budget. Runs once per registrable domain.
2. Default module subdomains_passive: THC ip.thc.org (community tier; terms open, reviewed by legal_dpo: free service, no contractual bar). Its POST lookup returns {domains:[{domain,last_seen_on}], next_page_state}: loop while next_page_state is set (at most 10 pages, max_requests 10), honour x-ratelimit-remaining, 429 = RATE_LIMITED, last_seen_on flags stale hosts.
3. Opt-in module subdomains_passive_extra (default_enabled=False, unknown tier until a promotion is recorded, excluded at AMBER and above): AnubisDB (anubisdb.com), Subdomain Center, subdomain.app and ScanMalware. myssl.com (CN operator, terms not reviewed) and RapidDNS (HTML scraping) are separate toggles with terms 'unknown' until legal records them; RapidDNS needs a recorded ToS/robots review.
4. Registry terms per feed (A1 BR3): THC, AnubisDB and Subdomain Center are 'open' (legal_dpo: free services with no published terms, no contractual bar); subdomain.app and ScanMalware get entries recorded by legal with evidence before they run; myssl.com and RapidDNS stay 'unknown' until legal records them (RapidDNS after the ToS/robots review).
5. Every name goes through the shared hostname sink of P001 and is merged with ct_logs into ONE deduped subdomain attribute with per-name provenance. Per-source cap 5000 names; partial flags honoured (ScanMalware degraded/partial/stale/subdomains_truncated, subdomain.app total > count, myssl code != 0).
6. Wildcard detection through ctx.dns.wildcard (P029): resolve two random 16-char labels and compare answers (Cloudflare NOERROR/NODATA quirk); names resolving only to wildcard answers are dropped. DoH resolution is capped at 300 names per run; the rest stay unresolved with expand=False.
7. Names seen only in one unknown-tier or noisy source (AnubisDB, Subdomain Center) are 'unconfirmed' unless they resolve (non-wildcard) or a second source agrees, and are never shown as assets otherwise. Names with an NXDOMAIN answer are labelled historical; DoH errors or timeouts leave them unresolved (could not verify, rule 5).
8. Only resolving, non-CDN-only names pivot (engine cap 12), prioritising telling labels (vpn, sso, mail, admin, dev, api). Discovered names get passive modules only. Names resolving to private, loopback or link-local addresses give a LOW 'internal address in public DNS' finding and are never contacted or pivoted.
9. Challenge pages (AnubisDB Cloudflare 'Attention Required', RapidDNS challenges) give BLOCKED reason_code 'challenge', are never retried with another UA or identity (A9), and lead to A3 demotion after 5 consecutive blocked runs. RapidDNS keeps min_interval >= 2 s and stops on Response.blocked.
10. Each adapter ships its own trimmed fixture (THC lookup, AnubisDB JSON and 403 page, Subdomain Center, subdomain.app...).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (redteam_lead voted P1). Tiers differ between members: legal_dpo rated AnubisDB and Subdomain Center community and ScanMalware unknown, osint_investigator the reverse. The A8 definitions require published terms for community tier, so the chair takes the stricter reading: only THC is community and default; the rest is opt-in and unknown until promoted. At the default TLP:AMBER unknown-tier sources are excluded anyway, so the outcome matches every member's intent. legal_dpo's floor: no CAPTCHA bypass, RapidDNS only after a ToS/robots review, myssl kept out. The architect's floor: per-source status and buckets, the shared sink, wildcard detection, caps, own fixtures. redteam_lead: internal-address findings and passive-only pivots. analyst_ux: one merged attribute, the budget and the label priority.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): One module with one small adapter per source (all return hostname lists); per-source token bucket and status (ok/empty/quota/blocked) recorded separately so one failing source never empties the result. Validate every name (PSL + IANA TLD, must end in .{domain}), record provenance per name (needed for 'only seen in CT/only from one source' rules), resolve via DoH before pivoting and apply wildcard detection. AnubisDB answers can contain junk: cap and validate. RapidDNS is HTML scraping: polite rate (>=2 s), stop on challenge pages. anubisdb.com is the live host (jldc.me errors).
Notes: Equivalent of subfinder's free source set without keys; complements CT logs for names that never had a public certificate.

## P020 `protective_dns` - Protective-DNS block check (Cloudflare Security/Families, AdGuard, Quad9, OpenDNS, CleanBrowsing)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Module protective_dns queries Cloudflare security.cloudflare-dns.com (1.1.1.2) and family.cloudflare-dns.com (1.1.1.3) through the JSON DoH layer, and Quad9 dns.quad9.net (RFC 8484 wire format). Registry: Cloudflare and Quad9 = open, tier trusted.
2. Opt-in module protective_dns_extra (default_enabled=False) holds AdGuard (dns.adguard-dns.com, family.adguard-dns.com), OpenDNS (doh.opendns.com) and CleanBrowsing. AdGuard and OpenDNS stay terms 'unknown' until legal records their terms; CleanBrowsing's free filters are 'for parents, not organizations' (noncommercial) and run only with a paid team account recorded as a licence.
3. Wire-format resolvers go only through dnspython (optional extra); without it those adapters report UNAVAILABLE reason_code 'package'. No hand-rolled DNS wire parser. ECS is never sent (P029). One query per resolver per name; cache only for the record TTL.
4. Each resolver endpoint declares a category: security (Quad9, Cloudflare 1.1.1.2, CleanBrowsing security, OpenDNS standard), family/adult (Cloudflare 1.1.1.3, AdGuard family, CleanBrowsing family, OpenDNS FamilyShield) or ads (AdGuard default). Only security-category blocks are maliciousness signals.
5. Blocked = the filtered answer matches the bundled per-resolver block-signature table (sinkhole/block-page IPs, 0.0.0.0, NXDOMAIN) AND the unfiltered core DoH reference resolves the name. The signature table ships as data with tests.
6. A block only by family/ads resolvers while the security sibling resolves gives an info tag 'content filter (adult/ads)' with zero risk; test: 1.1.1.3 blocks while 1.1.1.2 resolves gives no malicious finding.
7. Severity: one security resolver blocking = LOW 'blocked by <resolver>'; >= 2 = MEDIUM naming them. All protective-DNS votes count as ONE upstream family in P171 consensus (their feeds overlap), so HIGH comes only with another independent family.
8. Rule 5: no answer, REFUSED or timeout = 'could not verify <resolver>'. A full pass reads 'not blocked by N security resolvers at <time>', never 'clean'.
9. Tests with the recorded Cloudflare security/family and AdGuard family fixtures plus synthetic block-page, NXDOMAIN-with-reference and timeout cases.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (cti_lead P1, architect P3). legal_dpo's floor sets per-resolver terms: CleanBrowsing excludes organisations, OpenDNS and AdGuard need recorded terms, Cloudflare and Quad9 may run by default. The chair puts the not-yet-cleared resolvers in an opt-in module so the default module stays open and trusted (A3 BR2). The architect's floor: wire format only via dnspython, a bundled block-signature table, block only when the reference resolves. cti_lead and analyst_ux: only security-category blocks count, family/ads blocks are content context. Severity takes the lower of the members' ladders (cti_lead, osint_investigator) because P171 treats these resolvers as one family.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Query the same name through several filtering resolvers and compare with an unfiltered resolver: 0.0.0.0/NXDOMAIN/block-page IPs = 'blocked by <resolver>' (signal of malicious/phishing classification). JSON DoH works for Cloudflare/AdGuard; Quad9, CleanBrowsing and OpenDNS need RFC 8484 wire format (dnspython or own encoder); Quad9 :5053 is blocked behind HTTPS-only proxies. Never treat an unanswered resolver as 'clean'.
Notes: Cheap multi-vendor maliciousness signal for domains/URLs (web-check approach).

## P060 `archive_mining` - Archive mining techniques (waymore URL harvesting, historical robots.txt, historical favicons)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: url_web; group: G01; inputs: domain, url; produces: url, favicon_hash, analytics_id
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: web.archive.org, index.commoncrawl.org, otx.alienvault.com, urlscan.io, ghostarchive.org

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. A library on top of web_archives (P059): same client, same per-source status, archive hosts only. OTX, urlscan and ghostarchive are dropped from this module's hosts; their URL lists reach it through the graph from their own approved modules. IntelX via waymore stays off (P139 rules).
2. At most 20 archived bodies per run via id_ raw URLs, 1 MB each, fetched only from archive hosts and treated as untrusted data (never executed or rendered).
3. waymore filter/keyword lists (MIT) are bundled with attribution to keep interesting URLs (configs, backups, admin, API paths). Query-string secrets in harvested URLs are masked by P312 before storage.
4. Robofinder technique: robots.txt versions via CDX collapse=digest, parsed with a size cap; Disallow/Allow/Sitemap lines are unioned and disallowed paths become url entities with expand=False.
5. chronos technique: archived favicons are hashed with the shared pure-Python mmh3 helper (Shodan form: mmh3 over base64.encodebytes with the trailing newline, Shodan test vectors) and become favicon_hash pivots with expand=False.
6. Historical analytics ids and favicon hashes carry the snapshot date and are historical; the P061/P062 noise rules apply, and ids are never linked across eras without overlapping dates.
7. Harvested URLs, disallowed paths and endpoints are never requested from the live target outside the ACTIVE path module within scope.
8. Output: one grouped attribute 'interesting archived URLs' by category (config, backup, admin, API, robots Disallow), capped at 50 in the report; no per-URL findings except masked secrets from secret_scanner. default_enabled=False (opt-in).
9. Fixtures: CDX slices from web_archives plus one archived robots.txt and one archived favicon.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (cti_lead and analyst_ux voted P3). The architect's floor: a library over the archive client, archive hosts only, capped bodies, the shared mmh3 helper. legal_dpo's floor: P059 masking, no submissions anywhere, IntelX off, bodies kept as indicators. redteam_lead: harvested paths are never browsed. osint_investigator and cti_lead: dated historical ids. analyst_ux: grouped, capped output and opt-in. Archive hosts only: tier trusted, terms open.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Built on web_archives (no extra hosts): waymore filter/keyword lists (MIT, attribution) to keep interesting URLs (configs, backups, admin, API paths) from CDX/OTX/urlscan; Robofinder technique = robots.txt versions via CDX -> disallowed paths as url entities (expand=False); chronos technique = archived favicons -> mmh3 (mmh3 over base64.encodebytes with trailing newline, as Shodan) -> favicon_hash pivots. Fetch at most N archived bodies per run (default 20) and only from archive hosts, never from the target.
Notes: Recovers forgotten endpoints and historical favicons/analytics IDs for infrastructure pivots without touching the target.
