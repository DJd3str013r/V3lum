## P148 `greynoise_community` - GreyNoise Community API

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=noncommercial, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: threat_intel_ioc; group: G02; inputs: ipv4; produces: 
- auth: none; key env vars: V3LUM_GREYNOISE_KEY; hosts: api.greynoise.io

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Terms: the GreyNoise EULA (last modified 2025-05-07, verified 2026-11-26) offers free subscriptions 'for non-commercial purposes' (s.2.3) while s.3.1 allows 'internal business purposes or noncommercial purposes': the community tier is classed noncommercial.
2. Off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared: a company key plus a licence_allow entry (scope: internal defence, no resale or redistribution; written confirmation requested from GreyNoise) or a paid plan.
3. Key-first: key 'greynoise' (already registered) in the header. The keyless plan reports rate_limit '25-W' (25 lookups per week per egress IP) and a free key ~50/week: a weekly window in the P334 block (until it exists daily_quota=3), seeds (depth 0) only, never bulk pastes or pivots.
4. HTTP 404 'IP not observed scanning the internet' is a declared meaningful absence, worded 'not observed scanning by GreyNoise' (info), never 'benign'. 429 gives RATE_LIMITED ('could not verify'), shown once per scan with the free-key hint, never 'benign'.
5. riot=true: known_good tag with the service name ('Google Public DNS'); it dampens only that IP's reputation, never URLs or domains hosted on it, and caps blocklist-derived findings on that IP at LOW.
6. noise=true with benign classification: 'known benign scanner (<name>)', blocklist-derived score capped at LOW. noise=true with malicious classification: 'opportunistic mass scanner' at MEDIUM, explained as 'scans the whole internet, not targeting you specifically'.
7. riot/benign flags lower scores through named suppression rules (P322) but never delete other sources' findings. The viz.greynoise.io/ips/{ip} page is the manual link.
8. Tests with the recorded community fixture and the two 429 fixtures.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (cti_lead and analyst_ux voted P1). legal_dpo's floor classes the ambiguous free tier noncommercial (cti_lead and osint_investigator read the EULA the same way), so it is a licence-gated default candidate (A3 BR4) that shows up as coverage lost until legal records a decision. The architect's floor: key-first with a weekly window, 404/429 semantics. analyst_ux and cti_lead: riot/noise dampening limited to the IP and never deleting findings (osint_investigator). GreyNoise is an established company: tier trusted.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Keyless community endpoint with optional V3LUM_GREYNOISE_KEY; HTTP 404 = valid 'not observed' (empty), 429 = quota (anonymous limit hit from shared egress) -> rate_limited, never 'benign'. noise/riot flags -> scanner/known_good tags.
Notes: Separates internet background noise and benign services from targeted activity.

## P151 `isc_dshield` - SANS ISC / DShield IP API

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=noncommercial, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: threat_intel_ioc; group: G02; inputs: ipv4, asn; produces: asn
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: isc.sans.edu

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Terms: SANS ISC data is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (noncommercial, A1 BR15), but the ISC API page allows commercial use 'to protect your own company's network ... do not resell'. Off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared: legal records a licence_allow entry citing that clause (day-1 queue).
2. Allow-list scope: defence of the company's own networks only, no resale, no inclusion in paid deliverables to third parties.
3. Send Accept: application/json and ?json on /api/ip/{ip}, /api/asnum/{asn} and /api/threatcategory. A redirect to /blockme.html (detected from Response.url/history, not only body markers, P321) gives BLOCKED ('could not verify') with backoff.
4. ISC asks API users to identify themselves: the profile uses the identifying UA with the team role contact (setting contact_email, P326); without it the source is SKIPPED reason_code 'config'. No personal data is hard-coded.
5. min_interval >= 1 s, cache 24 h. Attack counts carry first/last seen; DShield sightings are opportunistic scanning: tag scanner, at most MEDIUM alone.
6. Attribution 'SANS ISC / DShield' in reports. Tests with the recorded IP, ASN and threat-feed fixtures plus a blockme redirect.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (cti_lead voted P1). legal_dpo, cti_lead and osint_investigator call it the model case for the licence allow-list: noncommercial by default, enabled under corporate only through a recorded own-network-defence entry. The architect's floor: Accept header, blockme detection from the final URL, a configured contact UA. cti_lead: opportunistic-scanning semantics. It remains a licence-gated default candidate (A3 BR4). SANS: tier trusted.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Send Accept: application/json (otherwise redirect to blockme.html with 200 -> check final URL) and an identifying UA (ISC asks for one); attribution; non-commercial wording -> confirm for corporate use; use /api/ip/{ip}?json, /api/asnum, /api/threatcategory. Gap fill: the ISC API page says 'It is ok to use this data for commercial purposes, for example to protect your own company's network ... do not resell' while the formal licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 -> candidate for a recorded legal allow-list entry (internal protection only) in the corporate licence profile.
Notes: Attack counts, first/last seen and threat feeds per IP.

## P274 `leakix` - LeakIX (exposed services and leaks, keyed)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=community, terms=key-terms, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: pentest_recon; group: G02; inputs: ipv4, domain, keyword; produces: domain
- auth: free_key; key env vars: V3LUM_LEAKIX_KEY; hosts: leakix.net

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Requires key 'leakix' (already registered), sent in the api-key header; 401 'Invalid API key' gives NEEDS_KEY. Key-terms: a company/team account on a plan recorded by legal before it runs under corporate; free 3,000 requests go into the P334 budget.
2. LeakIX is a sensitive provider: a field ALLOWLIST at ingestion, before res.raw and the HTTP cache (P145 schema): host, port, protocol, path, event type, plugin/leak type, severity and first/last seen. 'summary' and any leak, dataset or excerpt content are dropped; a test asserts the evidence JSON contains no summary text from the fixture.
3. Metadata only: leaked content, credentials and database samples are never fetched, stored or printed (rule 3).
4. Runs on seeds and org_profile own assets; events on pivoted third-party assets are recorded as counts only (existence, type, severity counts). Exposed database/config findings on org_profile assets are HIGH, with the LeakIX page as manual link.
5. Events older than 90 days are historical ('re-verify'). A leak is never confirmed by fetching the exposed resource from the target outside an ACTIVE authorized scope, and then only status and size, never contents.
6. Hits on CDN/shared IPs (P034) are attributed to the hostname, not to the IP owner.
7. Merge only with a trimmed real success fixture captured with the team key, plus the recorded 401 fixtures.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (architect and analyst_ux voted P3). The architect's floor and redteam_lead: LeakIX summaries embed the exposed data, so an allowlist scrub runs before the cache and evidence. legal_dpo's floor: key-terms, metadata only, no leaked content. cti_lead and osint_investigator: own assets first, counts for third parties, 90-day history, HIGH for own exposed databases. Key-required sources may be default-enabled (A3 BR5). LeakIX is a small identifiable operator: tier community.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Now key-only (401 without key; V3LUM_LEAKIX_KEY); host and subdomains endpoints; report leak existence/type only, never fetch leaked content or credentials.
Notes: Shows exposed databases/configs on organisation assets.
