## P346 `mobile_app_impersonation` - Brand app discovery and look-alike apps (iTunes Search, F-Droid index, Koodous, Digital Asset Links cert check)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=community, terms=license, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: url_web; group: G03; inputs: company_name, keyword, domain; produces: keyword, sha256, domain
- auth: mixed; key env vars: V3LUM_KOODOUS_TOKEN; hosts: itunes.apple.com, f-droid.org, developer.koodous.com, digitalassetlinks.googleapis.com
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0030": ["P008"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Brand terms come only from org_profile brands, or from a company_name/keyword seed the analyst typed with explicit -M; never from arbitrary domain pivots.
2. Apple iTunes Search/Lookup (country=br, entity=software): Apple's Search API terms cover promotional use, so this source is 'license': off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared (legal records a metadata-only scope in licence_allow).
3. App metadata only (name, seller, bundle id, URL); artwork and previews are never downloaded or displayed. <= 20 calls/min (min_interval 3 s); a 403 is retried once with the same UA, then BLOCKED ('could not verify'), never with another UA (P326).
4. Android official apps are confirmed through the digital_asset_links client of P008 (package + signing-certificate SHA-256, no copy of the client) and, for analyst-provided APK files, the apk_triage certificate SHA-256. F-Droid is dropped from this build (multi-MB index parsed in RAM, low relevance for banking brands).
5. Koodous only with a company token (key 'koodous', terms recorded, real fixture), for APK hash/package lookups; keyless 401 gives NEEDS_KEY. Google Play only as manual pivot links, never scraped.
6. Official apps (seller name/URL/bundle prefix matching org_profile) form a known_good list. Look-alike = brand term in the app name AND seller/developer not matching org_profile AND absent from the official domain's Digital Asset Links, scored by name similarity and seller mismatch.
7. Look-alikes are a review list at LOW ('needs analyst review'), capped at 20 in the report, pivots expand=False, never 'malicious' without analyst review.
8. .apk links found on scam pages or in pasted messages are recorded as url entities only and never downloaded automatically.
9. Tests with the recorded gaps__app_store_search and Digital Asset Links fixtures: official app recognised, look-alike listed for review, 403 handling.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (architect voted P3). legal_dpo's floor: Apple's terms are promotional, so iTunes use is metadata-only under a recorded scope (terms license, a licence-gated default candidate per A3 BR4), Koodous with a company token, Google Play never scraped. The architect's floor: rate limits, the P008 client, F-Droid dropped. cti_lead, osint_investigator and analyst_ux: the look-alike definition, a capped review list and APK links never downloaded. The synthesizer's 'normal client UA' is replaced by the default UA (P326 forbids changing the UA after a 403). Tier community (Koodous; Apple and Google are trusted).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Brand terms come from org_profile (or the analyst); iTunes Search (country=br, entity=software, <= 20 calls/min, normal client UA, 403 = could not verify with one backoff retry) -> official apps = seller name/URL/bundle prefix matching the org profile; other apps using brand terms = look-alike candidates (medium confidence, analyst review). F-Droid index matched offline (weekly). Koodous only with a free token for APK hash/package lookups. Android official apps confirmed by Digital Asset Links of the official domain (package + signing-cert SHA-256, P008) and, for APK files, apk_triage cert SHA-256. Google Play only as manual pivot links (P083), never scraped. Apple's Search API terms (promotional use) to be confirmed by legal.
Notes: Fake banking/retail apps are a recurring Brazilian fraud vector that domain-only brand protection misses.
