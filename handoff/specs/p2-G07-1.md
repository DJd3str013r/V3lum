### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Module threat_intel_ioc/crowdsec_cti: GET https://cti.api.crowdsec.net/v2/smoke/{ip} with header x-api-key; key 'crowdsec' registered in config.KNOWN_KEYS (env V3LUM_KEY_CROWDSEC; V3LUM_CROWDSEC_KEY alias). Registry provider crowdsec: tier trusted, terms key-terms.

3. Quota: the free Community plan is 120 lookups per month (docs, 2026-11-26): a P334 monthly quota block (setting crowdsec_monthly_quota, default 120 or the recorded plan's limit). run_on='seeds' (analyst-typed IPs only, never pivots or bulk pastes); private/reserved and CDN/cloud noise IPs and IPs answered in the last 72 h are skipped (cache 72 h).

5. 6. CVEs from attack_details read 'attempted exploitation of CVE-X seen by CrowdSec', expand=False, and never trigger own-asset vulnerability rules (CR15).
7. Provider data is not redistributed: excluded from STIX/MISP/JSON exports shared outside the company (verdict, link and source name only; EULA internal use). Success, 404 and 429 fixtures are captured with the team key (only 403 bodies exist).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (cti_lead and the architect voted P1). legal_dpo's floor requires a SOC-compatible plan from a company account and no redistribution; cti_lead found the 120-lookups-per-month limit, which makes the seeds-only rule and 72 h cache necessary. The architect's 404 = 'not in data' (never benign) and analyst_ux's CVE wording that avoids 'we are vulnerable' complete the spec.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Requires a free V3LUM_CROWDSEC_KEY (x-api-key); smoke/{ip} gives behaviours, attack details mapped to ATT&CK and CVEs, classifications (scanner/benign) -> ip -> cve -> technique graph; daily quota budget.
Notes: Best single attacker-IP API; plugs into the correlation graph.
