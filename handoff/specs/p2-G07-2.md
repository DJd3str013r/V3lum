## P349 `siem_search` - Internal SIEM look-back (approved saved searches on OpenSearch/Elasticsearch or Splunk)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=n/a, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: threat_intel_ioc; group: G07; inputs: ipv4, ipv6, domain, url, email, md5, sha256, username, user_agent; produces: 
- auth: free_key; key env vars: V3LUM_SIEM_URL, V3LUM_SIEM_TOKEN; hosts: -

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Module cti_framework/siem_search, default_enabled False, for the team's primary SIEM only in this build (A12), with a read-only token through `v3lum keys`, TLS verification on (ca_bundle) and disclosure 'internal'.
2. Only SOC-approved, versioned templates: OpenSearch/Elasticsearch JSON DSL term/terms queries on approved fields and index patterns (never query_string/Lucene syntax); Splunk only by dispatching SOC-approved saved searches with arguments, and only for values whose canonical form matches a strict charset (IP, domain, hash, e-mail); other types are refused.
3. Values enter templates only as parameters or escaped literals, tested with hostile values such as x" OR * | delete and a bare *.
4. Bounds: one query per entity, <= 20 queries per run, 30-day window by default, <= 1,000 results, 1 concurrent query, timeout 15 s by default and never above 30 s (A12); a failure means could not verify. Output: counts and first/last seen per entity only (no raw events), shown near the top ('seen in our logs: N events, first/last').
5. Results are also recorded as internal sightings (count, first/last seen, index pattern) and exported as sightings, never as raw events. Internal hostnames and usernames found are never auto-pivoted.
6. Workplace monitoring (LGPD): personal identifiers (username, e-mail, user_agent tied to a person) are searched only within a case with a recorded incident purpose, under the company's monitoring policy and privacy notice; queries are logged with analyst and case id; results follow case retention; never used for HR or performance purposes.
7. The covert-exercise flag of P348 also skips SIEM lookups. Synthetic fixtures for both backends.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2, per A12. The architect's floor (JSON DSL term queries only, strict-charset Splunk arguments) and redteam_lead's hostile-value tests make template injection impossible; legal_dpo's floor binds personal searches to a case purpose and the monitoring policy; cti_lead's sightings recording and analyst_ux's primary-SIEM scope and top-of-report line are kept. Internal platform: tier 'n/a'.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Off by default; only SOC-approved templated searches (no free-form query built from investigated values: injection/load risk), fixed index patterns and a 30-day default window, one query per entity, counts + first/last seen only (no raw events in V3LUM reports), read-only service account, 'internal' disclosure class (TLP:RED OK); internal hostnames/usernames found are never auto-pivoted.
Notes: Connects external CTI to 'did this touch us?' without leaving the organisation.

## P350 `offline_hash_sets` - Offline hash sets (VirusShare MD5 corpus, MalwareBazaar SHA-256 export with Auth-Key, CIRCL hashlookup Bloom prefilter)

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=n/a, terms=license, opsec=passive, shares_input=False
- category: malware_hash; group: G07; inputs: md5, sha1, sha256, file; produces: 
- auth: mixed; key env vars: V3LUM_ABUSECH_KEY; hosts: virusshare.com, urlhaus.abuse.ch, threatfox.abuse.ch, bazaar.abuse.ch, feodotracker.abuse.ch, sslbl.abuse.ch, cra.circl.lu, hashlookup.circl.lu
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0332": ["P156"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Offline module malware_hash/offline_hash_sets (disclosure none, TLP:RED-safe). This build: the MalwareBazaar v2 recent export (https://mb-api.abuse.ch/v2/files/exports/{AUTH_KEY}/recent.csv, last 48 h) with key 'abusech' through the dataset manager, refreshed at most hourly and indexed into SQLite; a hit reads 'known MalwareBazaar sample: <signature>, first seen <date>' (medium).
2. Licence: the MalwareBazaar export follows P156 ('license', abuse.ch terms s.4.1): off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared (a Spamhaus subscription or a written abuse.ch decision in licence_allow). The key-bearing URL passes AR01 redaction and is never logged, cached or shown.
3. Deferred inside this proposal: the VirusShare MD5 corpus (512 files, ~1.5 GB of text, stale since 2022) and the 1 GB CIRCL hashlookup Bloom filter, until the dataset manager offers a compact on-disk index (sorted 8-byte prefix file or mmap Bloom reader) behind an explicit size confirmation; neither is ever loaded into memory.
4. When added: VirusShare is a separate module (terms 'unknown' per A1 BR16, default_enabled False) worded 'present in the VirusShare corpus (historical; may include PUP/benign)', low, never malicious alone and never in CR27; the CIRCL Bloom (open data, 2023 snapshot) comes only from `v3lum data fetch hashlookup-bloom` with a 1 GB warning and is a prefilter, never a known_good verdict.
5. Downloads are verified (size, published hash when available) and stored under ~/.v3lum/datasets; the MalwareBazaar export is the one dataset registered for abusech (P156), never downloaded twice; dataset dates are shown in every finding and in coverage; misses mean nothing (no row).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2 (the architect voted P3). The architect's floor limits this build to the keyed MalwareBazaar recent export and defers the multi-GB corpora until compact on-disk indexes exist; legal_dpo's floor keeps the export under P156's licence gate and VirusShare (no licence) off until legal decides; cti_lead's, osint_investigator's and analyst_ux's wording keeps corpus membership from reading as a verdict. Chair-file terms follow the export ('license'); the module is a default candidate that is off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared (A3 BR4).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): `v3lum data fetch` builds local Bloom filters/sorted prefix files: VirusShare_00000-00511 MD5 lists (no key; stale since 2022; terms unknown -> research profile only until legal decides) = LOW 'present in VirusShare corpus'; MalwareBazaar v2 export (https://mb-api.abuse.ch/v2/files/exports/{AUTH_KEY}/recent.csv, full dump hourly at most; key never logged) = MEDIUM 'known MalwareBazaar sample' with signature; CIRCL hashlookup-full.bloom (1.0 GB, 2023 snapshot, optional) only as a prefilter to skip keyed lookups for probable known files, never a known_good verdict alone. All checks offline (shares_input False, TLP:RED OK); misses mean nothing.
Notes: Gives a keyless/offline first answer for hashes from EDR alerts and attachments instead of 'unknown'.
