## P220 `gleif` - GLEIF LEI API

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: company_org; group: G10; inputs: cnpj, company_name, keyword; produces: company_name
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: api.gleif.org
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0515": ["P361"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. One GLEIF client in a shared helper used by this module and by P361; no second client. Host api.gleif.org, keyless, CC0 (attribution courtesy line), documented 60 req/min: min_interval 1 s and a registry quota.
2. The registeredAs filter always sends the formatted CNPJ (entities.display_value, e.g. 00.000.000/0001-91); raw digits are never sent because they return 0 hits. Fixtures cover both forms.
3. Bounded: JSON:API page size <= 10 and at most 4 requests per entity (record, direct parent, ultimate parent, first page of children); children capped at 10.
4. Default scans use only CNPJ and exact company_name inputs. fuzzycompletions/fulltext (keyword) runs only when the module is selected explicitly, and its hits are hypotheses (expand=False, confidence <= 0.5).
5. Exact CNPJ/LEI matches pivot with relation partner/company_of; parents and children beyond one level are expand=False so a conglomerate cannot flood the graph. Results are ownership hints, never scope: they may be proposed by `v3lum org seed` for analyst confirmation but never extend org_profile or an ACTIVE scope by themselves.
6. Attributes: LEI, legal name, registration status (ISSUED/LAPSED/RETIRED), last update, direct and ultimate parent, shown on one line. No record is INFO 'no LEI' (most Brazilian SMEs have none), never a risk signal.
7. Rule 5: 401/403/429, timeouts, 5xx and error-in-200 bodies mean could not verify (P321/P328 statuses: BLOCKED, RATE_LIMITED, QUOTA, ERROR), never EMPTY or 'not found'; such responses are never cached. 404 is a declared meaningful absent status (absent_statuses {404}).
8. Merge gate (A16): offline tests with FakeHttp (unmatched requests fail, network guard active) over trimmed real fixtures without personal data (the 7 trimmed GLEIF captures), a provider-registry entry for every host, the registry contract test and the P327 lint.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2. CC0 legal-entity data with no legal objection (legal_dpo). The architect's conditions (one shared client with P361, formatted CNPJ only, request caps, fuzzy hits as hypotheses) and analyst_ux's one-line output with no fuzzy keyword noise in default scans are adopted. cti_lead's expansion limit and 'no LEI is not a risk signal' and redteam_lead's 'ownership hints, never scope' complete the spec.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): lei-records?filter[entity.registeredAs]= with the FORMATTED CNPJ (raw digits -> 0 hits), legalName filters and fuzzycompletions; parent/child relationships -> partner/company_of pivots; CC0.
Notes: Corporate ownership hierarchy and LEI.

8. CEP UF vs DDD vs CNPJ UF mismatches are weak info attributes for correlation; they never raise the score on their own (people and companies move).
9. Cache TTL 30 days (bounded by cache_max_age_days and retention_days).
10. Merge gate (A16): offline tests with FakeHttp (unmatched requests fail, network guard active) over trimmed real fixtures without personal data (the 9 captured BrasilAPI, ViaCEP, OpenCEP and IBGE responses plus a ViaCEP erro body), a provider-registry entry for every host, the registry contract test and the P327 lint.
