## P022 `whois_history_keyed` - WHOIS history / reverse WHOIS (WhoisXML, Whoxy, WhoisFreaks)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=community, terms=license, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: domain_dns; group: G01; inputs: domain, email, company_name, person_name; produces: domain, email, company_name
- auth: mixed; key env vars: V3LUM_WHOISXML_KEY, V3LUM_WHOXY_KEY, V3LUM_WHOISFREAKS_KEY; hosts: whoisxmlapi.com, whois-history.whoisxmlapi.com, reverse-whois.whoisxmlapi.com, ip-netblocks.whoisxmlapi.com, subdomains.whoisxmlapi.com, api.whoxy.com, api.whoisfreaks.com

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. One adapter per provider (WhoisXML API, Whoxy, WhoisFreaks), each with its own key in config.KNOWN_KEYS, status and credit budget (P334 monthly window). Company-paid keys only; legal records the licence/purchase in licence_allow before an adapter runs under corporate. WhoisXML and Whoxy take keys in the URL: only through the central redactor (AR01).
2. default_enabled=False. Never runs on auto-pivoted entities: run_on='seeds' or explicit -M selection. Results are candidates, never scope.
3. Reverse WHOIS by registrant e-mail or name only when that registrant value came from a WHOIS/RDAP record already in the case (no free-text person search), the analyst selected it, and the case has a recorded purpose (fraud/actor infrastructure). Never people-search (rule 4).
4. Privacy/proxy and redacted registrant values (the P003 privacy_proxy list, placeholder e-mails with privacy, proxy, redacted, whoisguard or contact-form patterns) are never used as reverse-WHOIS selectors or for CR32 clustering.
5. Minimise at ingestion: keep domain, registrar, dates and the matching field; drop other personal fields. Natural-person values are kept only as the link between hostile assets, tagged privacy and kept under case retention (A10).
6. Historical records carry their record date; pre-2018 (pre-GDPR) data is labelled historical. CR32 confidence follows the match field: e-mail > organisation > name.
7. cache_ttl 30 days (within cache_max_age_days); person-name/e-mail queries are removable with the cache purge. Error bodies map to NEEDS_KEY (Whoxy {status:0,'Invalid API Key'}, WhoisFreaks 400 'Please provide apikey') or QUOTA (credits exhausted).
8. Each adapter merges with a trimmed real success fixture captured with the team key, plus its error fixture.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P3 (osint_investigator voted P2). legal_dpo's floor: company-paid keys with recorded licence terms, off by default, reverse WHOIS on persons only inside a case with a purpose, minimisation, privacy tag and retention. osint_investigator tightened the selector rule (only registrant values already found in the case). The architect's floor: one adapter per provider with budgets, seeds only, redaction for URL keys, real fixtures. cti_lead: privacy-proxy exclusion and historical labels. Tier community (Whoxy and WhoisFreaks are small companies; WhoisXML is trusted); terms license (paid credits).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Paid/credit-based keys only (V3LUM_WHOISXML_KEY, V3LUM_WHOXY_KEY, V3LUM_WHOISFREAKS_KEY); disabled by default; reverse WHOIS by person name/email only inside an open case and only for registrant data already public; minimise personal registrant data (LGPD); cache 30 d to save credits.
Notes: Reverse WHOIS clusters typosquats/actor infrastructure (CR32 shared_registrant_cluster).

## P023 `ooni` - OONI censorship measurements per domain

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: domain_dns; group: G01; inputs: domain, url; produces: 
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: api.ooni.io

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Keyless OONI measurements API (api.ooni.io measurements and aggregation, ?domain=&probe_cc=&limit=), probe_cc=BR by default plus the case country; pagination via metadata.next_url, capped at 2 pages.
2. Context only: anomalies or confirmed blocking per country (court- or ANATEL-ordered blocks, DNS tampering) are shown as info and never contribute to the risk score.
3. OONI data is reused with attribution (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and the report footer when used). The research did not confirm the exact Creative Commons variant: legal records it with evidence before merge, and if it carries a NonCommercial clause the source is classed noncommercial and is off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared.
4. Opt-in: default_enabled=False; runs with -M ooni or --preset deep. Errors and timeouts = could not verify.
5. Tests with the recorded measurements and aggregation fixtures.

Chair rationale: Approved 4-0 with two defers (cti_lead, analyst_ux: low signal for a CTI cell) at median P3. osint_investigator: context only, BR probes by default. legal_dpo: attribution under the OONI data licence. The architect found it bounded and fixture-backed. Opt-in as drafted. OONI is an established non-profit measurement project: tier trusted, terms open (CC data with attribution, queried at runtime).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Keyless measurements API (?domain=&probe_cc=BR&limit=); report anomalies/blocking by country as context only; opt-in (low CTI value).
Notes: Explains reachability differences (blocked in BR, DNS tampering).
