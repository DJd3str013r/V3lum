## P051 `ip_geolocation` - Keyless IP geolocation chain (ipwho.is, FreeIPAPI, ip.guide, IP2Location.io, GeoJS, KeyCDN)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=community, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: ip_network; group: G02; inputs: ipv4, ipv6; produces: asn
- auth: none; key env vars: V3LUM_IP2LOCATION_KEY; hosts: ipwho.is, free.freeipapi.com, freeipapi.com, ip.guide, api.ip2location.io, get.geojs.io, tools.keycdn.com

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Offline first: ip_offline_db (P047) answers first. This online chain runs only when the offline answer is missing or on explicit request (-M ip_geolocation or --preset deep), only on analyst-typed seed IPs (run_on='seeds'), never on pivot IPs, at most 2 providers per IP.
2. Bogon, RFC 1918, CGNAT, loopback and link-local addresses are filtered before any call. IPs from internal logs, e-mails, customers or employees (personal data) use the offline data only.
3. Wired now (commercial use allowed): ipwho.is (success:false + message in a 200 body = ERROR/QUOTA; connection.domain becomes an org-domain pivot with expand=False) and FreeIPAPI (free.freeipapi.com/api/v1/json/{ip}, 60 req/min; isProxy is a weak signal). Both community tier.
4. IP2Location.io keyless, GeoJS and ip.guide are added only after legal records their terms and tier (ip.guide serves JSON as text/plain: parse regardless of the header). KeyCDN is not wired: its mandatory UA 'keycdn-tools:https://<site>' would disclose the company's website on every lookup.
5. One shared vote helper with P046. Output: country and ASN/ISP with the agreeing sources ('BR - 2/2 providers'), labelled approximate; city only as 'approximate (<provider>)' with disagreements shown.
6. Geolocation is context only: never a risk factor by itself, never presented as a person's location, never a located_at/coordinates pivot.
7. 1 req/s per provider, cache 24 h, per-provider fixture and status. Tests with the recorded ipwho.is and FreeIPAPI fixtures and a success:false body.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0. The tally median is P3 on a 3-3 split (osint_investigator, redteam_lead and legal_dpo voted P2); the chair keeps P3 because offline data answers the question first. cti_lead, osint_investigator, the architect and analyst_ux require offline-first and no default fan-out to six providers, so it is opt-in. legal_dpo's floor: per-provider terms (only ipwho.is and FreeIPAPI state commercial use), offline only for personal IPs, never a person's location. redteam_lead's removal of KeyCDN is adopted because its UA identifies the company (stricter than legal_dpo's off-by-default). The architect: shared vote helper, bogon filter, error-in-200 handling.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Filter bogons/RFC1918 before any call. Provider chain with fallback and majority vote on country (geo is approximate, show source): ipwho.is (commercial allowed on free tier; success:false in 200 body = error), FreeIPAPI (moved to free.freeipapi.com/api/v1/json/{ip}), ip.guide (text/plain JSON), IP2Location.io keyless tier, GeoJS, KeyCDN (requires UA 'keycdn-tools:https://<site>' per its docs). 1 req/s per provider, cache 24 h.
Notes: Country/city/ISP context without keys; consensus avoids single-provider errors.

## P052 `ipqualityscore` - IPQualityScore (IP/URL/email/phone reputation, keyed)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=trusted, terms=key-terms, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: ip_network; group: G02; inputs: ipv4, url, email, phone; produces: 
- auth: free_key; key env vars: V3LUM_IPQS_KEY; hosts: ipqualityscore.com

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. veto_lift: accepts = {ipv4, url} only; e-mail and phone are removed from accepts and produces. The e-mail and phone validation endpoints are never wired: a unit test asserts no request to /api/json/email or /api/json/phone can be built (provider-side mailbox/carrier probing = rule 2 by proxy).
2. veto_lift: URL reputation runs only for URLs tagged hostile (malicious/phishing/suspicious), never for internal, confidential or org_profile URLs, and is skipped at TLP:AMBER and above: the URL source carries a source-level reuses_queries flag in the registry, so core/policy.py excludes it at AMBER+ (Status.SKIPPED reason_code 'tlp'). It is also flagged fetches_target.
3. veto_lift: a company-owned key with IPQS terms recorded by legal (key-terms, key_plans.ipqs) before it runs under corporate. Key 'ipqs' in config.KNOWN_KEYS.
4. veto_lift: success:false in a 200 body ('Invalid or unauthorized key', quota messages) maps through a P321 marker to NEEDS_KEY, QUOTA or ERROR, never EMPTY.
5. IPQS puts the key in the URL path (/api/json/ip/{key}/{ip}): merge only after the central redaction (AR01) masks configured key values anywhere in URLs, path included, in the cache, events/live feed, logs and evidence.
6. Monthly budget (1,000 free lookups) through the P334 block. default_enabled=False. The fraud score is one vote, never 'malicious' alone. The registry and core/policy.py (P327) support this source-level flag overriding the provider's, with a policy-table test.
7. Merge only with a trimmed real success fixture captured with the company key, plus the recorded invalid-key fixture.

Chair rationale: Vetoed by legal_dpo (5-1 otherwise): as drafted it accepted e-mail and phone, and IPQS validation of those probes mailboxes and carriers (rule 2). veto_lift adopted: legal_dpo's lift conditions match what every other member asked (cti_lead, osint_investigator, redteam_lead and analyst_ux all excluded the e-mail/phone endpoints; redteam_lead flagged the URL endpoint fetches_target). The chair implements the lift's AMBER+ exclusion of URL checks through a source-level reuses_queries flag, so the gate in core/policy.py enforces it. The architect's floor: path-key redaction and a real fixture. Approved at median P3, opt-in. IPQS is an established company: tier trusted.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Requires V3LUM_IPQS_KEY; HTTP 200 with success:false means error/quota -> check body; small free monthly quota -> budget; disabled by default.
Notes: Fraud scoring for IPs/emails/phones when the team has a key.
