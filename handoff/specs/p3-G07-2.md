- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=community, terms=unknown, opsec=passive, shares_input=True

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. One module per provider, each with its own key in config.KNOWN_KEYS, status, terms, quota and success fixture, all default_enabled False: triage (Recorded Future Triage, Bearer), metadefender (OPSWAT, apikey), malshare (api_key), polyswarm (Authorization) and virusshare (apikey; key required even though an empty key answered). Order: Triage, then MetaDefender, then the rest.
2. Per-provider path allow-lists with tests prove that no sample download (MalShare action=getfile, VirusShare download, Triage sample files, MetaDefender downloads) or submission endpoint is ever built: search/metadata only.
3. Licence (legal floor, verified 2026-11-26): Triage's free Individual/Researcher licences are non-commercial and make analyses public, so corporate use needs a commercial Triage licence; OPSWAT licenses free use 'solely for Your personal use', so MetaDefender needs a paid plan.
4. Classes: triage free 'noncommercial' (commercial licence 'license'); metadefender free 'noncommercial' (paid plan 'key-terms'); malshare, polyswarm and virusshare 'unknown' until legal records their terms. Each adapter runs under corporate only once its plan or licence is recorded; empty-key answers are ignored.
5. Triage: malware config extraction gives C2 IOCs as communicates_with pivots, hostile-tagged for P376 and never contacted. Each adapter is one vote; same-vendor sources (MetaDefender and Filescan are both OPSWAT) count once in consensus (P171). Daily budgets via P334.
6. Registry tiers: Recorded Future, OPSWAT and PolySwarm trusted; MalShare and VirusShare community. Each adapter ships only with a success fixture (today only VirusShare has one).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P3. The architect's floor splits the five providers into separate adapters with their own status, terms and fixtures; legal_dpo's verified terms make Triage and MetaDefender free tiers non-commercial and the others unknown until recorded, so all stay opt-in. cti_lead's Triage-first order, redteam_lead's no-download/no-submit allow-lists and the OPSWAT single-vote rule are kept.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Each adapter only with its own key (Triage Bearer, MalShare api_key, MetaDefender apikey, PolySwarm Authorization, VirusShare apikey - VirusShare answered with an empty key but treat it as key-required); search/metadata only, never download or submit samples; daily budgets.
Notes: Extra sandbox/AV coverage for unknown hashes.

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Depends on the P333 structural hash entity types (imphash, tlsh, telfhash, gimphash, authentihash, icon_dhash, code_signing_cert) and the P267/P271/P368 attributes; the relation similar_to is added to core/vocab.py in the same change.
2. Remote pivots: MalwareBazaar get_imphash/get_tlsh/get_telfhash/get_gimphash/get_dhash_icon/get_issuerinfo/get_subjectinfo with key 'abusech', limit <= 50, query_status no_results -> EMPTY; YARAify (P175) supplies the structural values of known samples.
3. Licence: the MalwareBazaar pivots and the Code Signing Certificate Blocklist (CSCB, from the keyed export via the dataset manager; the legacy URL is 410) follow P156 ('license'): off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared; YARAify (CC0) and local similarity run freely.
4. Results are hypotheses: relation similar_to with confidence 0.3-0.6 and expand=False, never a finding above low on their own, labelled 'hypothesis' in reports. A certificate on the CSCB is a malicious-signer finding (high).
5. Generic-value stoplist: imphash/telfhash/gimphash values seen across > 10 unrelated MalwareBazaar signatures or > 500 samples (e.g. the .NET imphash f34d5f2d4577ed6d9ceec516c1f5a744) and common installer/packer stubs are shown as 'common, not distinctive' and never pivoted.
6. Output is grouped per structural hash ('N samples share this imphash; families X (8), Y (3)'), top 10 listed. Local TLSH/ssdeep distance between files of one case is a correlation rule over case attributes, not a per-entity module; TLSH comparison waits for a wheel-providing implementation.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P3. legal_dpo's floor puts the MalwareBazaar parts under P156's licence gate while YARAify and local similarity run; cti_lead's and redteam_lead's stoplist, the architect's correlation-rule split for cross-file similarity and analyst_ux's grouped output are consolidated. A default candidate that is off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared for the MalwareBazaar parts (A3 BR4).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Requires the new entity types (P333 extension) and attributes from pe_triage/file_hashing/elf_triage; remote pivots only with the abuse.ch Auth-Key (MalwareBazaar get_imphash/get_tlsh/get_telfhash/get_gimphash/get_dhash_icon/get_issuerinfo/get_subjectinfo, limit <= 50, query_status no_results is not an error); YARAify lookup_hash (keyless) supplies the structural values of known samples; local TLSH/ssdeep distance between files of the same case; results are hypotheses (relation similar_to, confidence 0.3-0.6, expand=False); code-signing certificate blocklist (CSCB) from the Auth-Key export, legacy URL is 410.
Notes: Clusters samples from the same campaign using data V3LUM already computes.
