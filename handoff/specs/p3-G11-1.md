## P294 `d3fend` - MITRE D3FEND (ATT&CK -> defensive techniques)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=True, provider_tier=trusted, terms=open, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: cti_framework; group: G11; inputs: mitre_technique; produces: 
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: d3fend.mitre.org

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Prefer the D3FEND ATT&CK mapping export as a bulk dataset through the dataset manager (version recorded), matched locally (disclosure 'none', works at TLP:RED), over one API call per technique.
2. If the per-technique API (d3fend.mitre.org/api/offensive-technique/attack/{T}.json) is used as a fallback: only the technique id is sent, cache 30 days, and 404 means 'no mapping', never an error.
3. Output: at most 5 countermeasures per technique in a 'recommended controls' section (INFO/recommendations only) with links to d3fend.mitre.org/offensive-technique/attack/{id}/.
4. Licence (legal floor): reproduce MITRE's D3FEND copyright designation and licence text in data/NOTICE, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES and every report that shows D3FEND content (Terms of Use checked 2026-11-26: royalty-free for research, development and commercial purposes with the notice).
5. Merge gate: a trimmed real fixture of the mapping export (and of one API answer if the fallback is kept) captured before merge, since research captured none (architect, analyst_ux).

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P3 (legal_dpo, the architect and analyst_ux voted P2). cti_lead and the architect prefer the bulk mapping matched offline; legal_dpo's notice requirement and analyst_ux's 5-control cap are adopted, and the missing fixture is a merge gate.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): d3fend.mitre.org/api/offensive-technique/attack/{T}.json -> countermeasures for report recommendations; cache 30 d.
Notes: Turns findings into recommended defensive controls.

## P296 `malpedia` - Malpedia (families, actors, references)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=trusted, terms=noncommercial, opsec=passive, shares_input=True
- category: cti_framework; group: G11; inputs: malware_family, threat_actor, keyword; produces: malware_family, threat_actor
- auth: none; key env vars: V3LUM_MALPEDIA_TOKEN, V3LUM_MALPEDIA_KEY; hosts: malpedia.caad.fkie.fraunhofer.de

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Licence (A1 initial class, legal floor): Malpedia content is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, class 'noncommercial': off under the corporate profile until licence-cleared. The licence_allow entry requires a written Fraunhofer FKIE agreement ('alternative agreements' per its ToS); under a recorded research basis it runs with the licence banner.
2. Endpoint allow-list in code: list/families, find/actor, get/actor and get/family only. The URL builder rejects /api/get/sample/* and every sample, YARA or other download endpoint by construction (unit test on the module's URL set; P327 lint); no binary ever reaches the analyst machine.
3. Optional team-account token in the Authorization header only. Non-public material from the token tier carries the source TLP:AMBER, stays inside the case, never appears in lower-TLP reports and is never exported.
4. Under the corporate profile, family/actor ids and reference URLs come from the bundled MISP galaxy Malpedia cluster (P295) and joins on Malpedia ids from ThreatFox/galaxy work without this API; this module only adds live detail. Manual Malpedia pages stay available through P300.
5. Caching: list/families 7 days; actor ids are lowercase slugs (apt28, lazarus_group) and family ids '<platform>.<name>'. Opt-in (default_enabled=False).
6. Merge gate (A16): offline tests with FakeHttp (unmatched requests fail, network guard active) over trimmed real fixtures without personal data (the 6 Malpedia captures), a provider-registry entry for every host, the registry contract test and the P327 lint.

Chair rationale: Approved 5-0-1 at median P3 (analyst_ux deferred until legal clears the non-commercial licence). legal_dpo's floor and the binding A1 class make it noncommercial with a written FKIE agreement as the path. The architect's and redteam_lead's sample-download prohibition is enforced by construction. cti_lead's and osint_investigator's galaxy fallback keeps the value under the corporate profile.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Content CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (non-commercial!) and some material TLP:AMBER -> council must approve corporate use; list/families cached, find/actor and get/family on demand; optional token for non-public material.
Notes: Authoritative malware family catalogue (ThreatFox uses its ids).
