### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Two modules over one pure-Python mmh3 core helper (Shodan test vector: Google favicon -> 708578229; mmh3 over base64.encodebytes with the trailing newline), reused by archive_mining, cl0q and the engine pivot links. Also MD5 and SHA-256 of the bytes.
2. favicon_hash (PASSIVE, default): hashes favicon bytes from archived snapshots (Wayback id_ via the P059 client) or existing urlscan results, and hashes typed favicon_hash values into engine pivot links. No contact with the target.
3. favicon_fetch (LIGHT, default_enabled=False): one GET of the icon on analyst-typed seeds only (never pivots), after the entity's passive lane, through the P376 egress guard (hostile hosts only via a non-corporate egress, fail closed), counted in the P334 LIGHT budget. Not merged or enabled before the AR20 safety suite passes.

5. A bundled list of default/blank favicon hashes (web servers, CMS, hosting panels, parking pages, routers), plus hashes reported on > 1,000 hosts, marks noise: recorded, never pivoted.
6. Engine pivot links (Shodan, FOFA, Criminal IP, Netlas, ZoomEye) by default; automatic reverse queries only through enabled keyed engine modules. Reverse matches are hypotheses: relation similar_favicon, expand=False, never eligible for an ACTIVE scope.
7. Tests with the recorded design_pivots_and_js fixture, the Shodan test vector, a default-favicon suppression and a hostile href to a private IP that the safe-fetch helper refuses.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P2. cti_lead, osint_investigator and redteam_lead require passive bytes first and the live fetch only as LIGHT behind the egress guard; A3 BR2 makes LIGHT opt-in, so the chair splits the passive hashing (default) from the live fetch (opt-in). The passive module reads from the Internet Archive: tier trusted, terms open.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Fetch /favicon.ico or <link rel=icon> (light mode); mmh3.hash(base64.encodebytes(data)) with the trailing newline (Shodan compatible; pure-python murmur3 verified: Google favicon -> 708578229); emit favicon_hash entity + Shodan/FOFA/Criminal IP/Netlas/ZoomEye pivot links; skip default/blank favicons.
Notes: Finds origin IPs behind CDNs and cloned phishing kits (CR29).

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Split by opsec (architect floor). unshorten_preview (PASSIVE, default): documented no-click preview pages (TinyURL preview, Bitly '+' info page), only for shortener providers that offer one and whose registry tier is trusted or community with recorded terms; other shorteners on the bundled list are left to unshorten_trace. A challenge or login page gives BLOCKED and the manual link (A9).
2. unshorten_me (default_enabled=False): the unshorten.me JSON API is unknown tier (excluded at AMBER and above) with its 10 new lookups/hour per IP as a P334 quota; its service fetches the short link, so its source is flagged fetches_target.
3. unshorten_trace (LIGHT, default_enabled=False; not merged or enabled before the AR20 safety suite passes): analyst-typed seeds only, never confidential client URLs. A direct HEAD/GET of a short link counts as a click visible to the link creator's analytics.
4. The tracer uses the core safe-fetch helper: allow_redirects=False loop, <= 10 hops, http/https only, ports 80/443 unless explicit, every hop's host resolved and refused when private, loopback, link-local, CGNAT or metadata (169.254.169.254), 256 KB body cap, no cookies, no JS, HEAD then GET. Every hop is recorded (redirects_to) and written to the request ledger.
5. By default the tracer contacts only hosts on the bundled shortener/redirector list and reads the Location header; the first non-shortener destination is recorded uncontacted unless the analyst passes --follow-final, which goes through the P376 egress guard (hostile hosts only via a non-corporate egress, fail closed).
6. URLs carrying a probable per-recipient token (an org_profile e-mail in plain, URL-encoded or base64 form, or opaque tokens of >= 16 chars) are never contacted automatically and need a per-URL confirmation (A7 BR15).
7. Bundled shortener list covering services common in BR smishing (encurtador.com.br, cutt.ly, rb.gy, is.gd, shorturl.at, t.co, bit.ly, tinyurl...), with fixture tests. Hop URLs are masked for e-mails and tokens before evidence.
8. The final URL becomes a url pivot for the passive reputation modules. Tests: redirects to 127.0.0.1 and to an RFC 1918 address are refused; a token URL is not contacted; the unshorten.me fixture parses.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0. The tally median is P2 on a 3-3 split (cti_lead, osint_investigator and analyst_ux voted P1 for BR smishing triage); the chair keeps P2 because the passive previews deliver most of the value at low effort. The architect's floor splits passive previews from the LIGHT tracer and makes every hop SSRF-checked. cti_lead and osint_investigator: unshorten.me is unknown tier, and self-following is a visible click, hence LIGHT. redteam_lead: contact only shorteners and stop before the landing page unless asked, never contact token URLs. legal_dpo: masked hops and the quota. Notes: Safely reveals where phishing short links land.
