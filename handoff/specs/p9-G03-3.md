## P078 `url_scanners_third_party` - Third-party URL scanners (Sucuri SiteCheck, Web-Check, CheckPhish, urlDNA, urlquery, Lookyloo)

- council: **approved**, priority P3, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=community, terms=key-terms, opsec=light, shares_input=True
- category: url_web; group: G03; inputs: url, domain; produces: url, domain, ipv4
- auth: mixed; key env vars: V3LUM_CHECKPHISH_KEY, V3LUM_URLDNA_KEY, V3LUM_URLQUERY_KEY; hosts: sitecheck.sucuri.net, web-check.as93.net, developers.checkphish.ai, api.urldna.io, api.urlquery.net, lookyloo.circl.lu

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. veto_lift: search/cached-result endpoints only: urlDNA search, urlquery report search and Lookyloo (CIRCL) lookups of existing captures. No submission or scan code path exists in this module; a test asserts no request to any submit/scan endpoint (urlDNA scan, urlquery submit, Lookyloo /submit) can be built.
2. Sucuri SiteCheck, Web-Check and CheckPhish are not implemented: Sucuri's only endpoint starts a scan when no recent result exists, CheckPhish only scans, and Web-Check checks make its server fetch or port-scan the target (api/ports.js connects to 32 ports). They are third-party touches of the target.
3. veto_lift: any future submission module may send URLs only to providers whose result is documented as non-public (private, unlisted and not shared with other customers), otherwise never; only for URLs tagged hostile, with an explicit per-URL flag; never confidential client URLs or URLs embedding personal data; skipped at TLP:AMBER and above. Recorded in BACKLOG.md; not built now.
4. veto_lift: company-owned keys (urldna, urlquery in config.KNOWN_KEYS) with terms recorded by legal (key-terms). Missing keys give NEEDS_KEY (urlDNA 422 'authorization header field required', urlquery 401 empty body). Each adapter has its own status and budget.
5. Registry terms per source (A1 BR3): urlDNA and urlquery are key-terms (company keys, plans recorded by legal). The keyless Lookyloo lookups of CIRCL's public instance (trusted) run once legal records CIRCL's terms for that instance with evidence (legal_dpo's lift lists Lookyloo capture lookup as allowed).
6. Results are third-party verdicts shown as attributes with scan dates (> 30 days = historical), one vote in consensus, never ground truth. E-mails and tokens in result URLs are redacted by the core helper before evidence.
7. default_enabled=False. Each adapter merges with a trimmed real success fixture, plus the recorded no-key fixtures.

Chair rationale: Vetoed by legal_dpo (5-1 otherwise): as drafted it allowed submissions to services whose captures may be public (rule 7). veto_lift adopted, in a stricter form: the architect's floor ships only the search/cached-result part in this build, and redteam_lead showed that Sucuri's 'cached result' starts a scan when no recent one exists and that Web-Check port-scans the target, so those adapters are dropped. cti_lead and osint_investigator: rule 7 without exception for hostile URLs. analyst_ux: explicit gates for any submission. legal_dpo's submission conditions become the BACKLOG unblock rule. Approved at median P3 (redteam_lead voted P2), opt-in. Tier community (urlDNA, urlquery are small companies; CIRCL is trusted).

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Search/cached-result endpoints first (urlDNA search, urlquery reports, Lookyloo recent captures, Sucuri cached last_scan). Any call that makes the provider's crawler visit the target (Sucuri scan, Web-Check checks, CheckPhish scan, Lookyloo /submit) is a third-party touch: allowed only for clearly hostile URLs (phishing triage) or authorized assets, explicit flag, never for client confidential URLs; Lookyloo/urlscan-style captures may be public -> publishes_submissions. Keys: V3LUM_URLDNA_KEY, V3LUM_URLQUERY_KEY, V3LUM_CHECKPHISH_KEY; CheckPhish returns errorMessage in 200.
Notes: Lets analysts see phishing pages without visiting them from the corporate network.

## P275 `tls_inspect` - TLS certificate inspection with interception detection

- council: **approved**, priority P1, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=n/a, terms=open, opsec=light, shares_input=False
- category: pentest_recon; group: G03; inputs: domain, url, ipv4; produces: domain, cert_fingerprint
- auth: none; key env vars: -; hosts: -
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0632": ["P277", "P329"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. Core ctx.tls handshake helper first (architect floor), shared with core/tls.py (P329): stdlib ssl through the core socket connector honouring the egress profile (CONNECT via the profile proxy, SOCKS via optional PySocks, names resolved through the profile), 10 s timeout, returning the DER chain; no raw socket leaves outside the profile.
2. LIGHT module: default_enabled=False, not merged or enabled before the AR20 safety suite passes. Analyst-typed seeds only, never pivots, after the entity's passive lane, through the P376 egress guard (hostile hosts only via a non-corporate egress, fail closed).
3. One handshake per host per run within the P334 LIGHT budget; protocol/cipher probing stays in the ACTIVE tls_protocol_scan. Public addresses only unless the IP is in an authorized scope. In passive mode certificate facts come from CT (P001) and indexed scan data.
4. Interception detection: when the system store verifies and a context loaded only with certifi's Mozilla bundle fails ('self-signed certificate in chain'), record intercepted_by=<issuer> per egress profile and suppress all certificate findings.
5. Suppression is never silent: a banner in terminal and HTML ('TLS interception by <issuer> detected on this network: certificate findings suppressed'), a coverage note, and doctor shows it (P329).
6. Analysis is a pure function over the chain; certificates are parsed with the optional 'cryptography' extra, with a stdlib getpeercert() fallback for chains that verify.
7. Hygiene findings (expired HIGH, < 14 d MEDIUM, < 30 d LOW, hostname mismatch HIGH, self-signed MEDIUM, RSA < 2048 / EC < 256 MEDIUM, SHA-1 MEDIUM) apply only to org_profile own assets and in-scope targets.
8. For other hosts they are info context; on hostile hosts cert facts are attributes (issuer, fingerprint for hunting), and phishing-relevant signals (cert issued < 7 days, free DV cert on a brand lookalike) feed correlations.
9. SANs become cert_san domain pivots; SANs outside the queried apex are expand=False. Certificates with > 20 SANs across > 5 registrable domains, or CDN/shared-hosting certificates (P034), give an attribute only and are excluded from CR31. The fingerprint becomes a cert_fingerprint pivot.
10. Tests use a FakeTls (tests/fakes.py) with real PEM fixtures, no network: an intercepted chain gives the banner and no cert findings; an expired own-asset certificate gives HIGH; the same certificate on a third-party host gives info.

Chair rationale: Approved 6-0 at median P1 (cti_lead and osint_investigator voted P2). A handshake is LIGHT, so the module is opt-in (A3 BR2) and gated on the safety suite (A16); passive certificate facts come from CT, as osint_investigator noted. The architect's floor: an injectable ctx.tls helper with FakeTls tests, a pure analysis function, the optional cryptography extra. redteam_lead: the socket must follow the egress profile, one handshake per host, seeds only. cti_lead, redteam_lead and analyst_ux: ownership-scoped severity, shared-certificate rule, visible interception banner. Target contact: tier n/a; stdlib/Apache code: terms open.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): One handshake (CONNECT through proxy when configured); verify with the system store and again with certifi's Mozilla bundle only: if the first verifies and the second fails with 'self-signed certificate in chain', the path is TLS-intercepted -> mark intercepted_by=<issuer> and suppress all cert findings (Céos antivirus problem; the sandbox proxy re-signed github.com). Findings: expired HIGH, <14 d MEDIUM, <30 d LOW, hostname mismatch HIGH, self-signed MEDIUM, RSA<2048/EC<256 MEDIUM, SHA-1 MEDIUM; SANs -> domain pivots (cert_san); fingerprint -> cert_fingerprint. Protocol downgrade probing is in tls_protocol_scan (active).
Notes: Certificate facts, expiry findings and SAN pivots; core input for CR17 and CR31.

## P347 `page_capture` - Headless page capture for evidence (Playwright screenshot/DOM/HAR) with urlscan screenshot reuse

- council: **approved**, priority P2, default_enabled=False, provider_tier=n/a, terms=open, opsec=light, shares_input=True
- category: url_web; group: G03; inputs: url, domain; produces: url, domain, ipv4
- auth: none; key env vars: V3LUM_URLSCAN_KEY; hosts: urlscan.io
- sources shared with other proposals: {"C0143": ["P066"]}

### Final conditions (binding spec - implement every item)

1. veto_lift (1): ships as the separate optional extra v3lum[capture]; nothing in v3lum/core imports playwright. Not merged or enabled before the AR20 safety suite passes.
2. veto_lift (2): refuses to start unless Chromium's sandbox is active (never --no-sandbox; abort when running as root without a usable sandbox) and a non-corporate egress proxy (P376 proxy_light) is configured; fail closed, never a fallback through the corporate IP.
3. veto_lift (3): every request goes through a route interceptor that aborts private, loopback, link-local, CGNAT, ULA and metadata destinations, intranet names (DNS rebinding) and non-http(s) schemes. DNS is resolved through the egress proxy.
4. veto_lift (4): downloads, permissions (geolocation, notifications, clipboard, camera, microphone), service workers, extensions and file:// are disabled, with a fresh ephemeral context and temporary profile per page, deleted afterwards. Hardening asserted by a launch-args test: WebRTC limited to proxied traffic (no non-proxied UDP), QUIC off, integrated Windows/Kerberos auth disabled.
5. veto_lift (5): hard budgets of 30 s, 25 MB HAR, one screenshot, <= 10 redirect hops and <= 150 requests per capture. No clicks, scrolling, typing, form submission or credentials.
6. veto_lift (6): runs only on explicit -M selection for analyst-typed seed URLs already tagged phishing/malicious (LIGHT, A7). URLs carrying a recipient identifier need an explicit per-capture override. No stealth or anti-detection plugins (rule 1); challenge pages (Turnstile/hCaptcha) end BLOCKED with no retry.
7. veto_lift (7): unit tests run against a fake driver interface, with a single @live test. veto_lift (8): reuse of public urlscan screenshots lives in urlscan_search (P066, passive) and works without playwright.
8. A benign-looking or cloaked capture reads 'inconclusive (possible cloaking/geofencing)', never evidence of benign. HAR and DOM are scrubbed of our cookies, auth headers and proxy credentials; victims' e-mails shown on pages are redacted in report text.
9. Evidence record: final URL, redirect chain, UTC and America/Sao_Paulo timestamps, SHA-256 of screenshot/DOM/HAR, the egress profile used, the case TLP; under case retention (A10) with access limited to the case. Screenshots are embedded in the self-contained HTML as JPEG data URIs (<= 1.5 MB) with a caption (source, egress, time).

Chair rationale: Vetoed by the architect (5-1 otherwise): a full browser engine against attacker pages executes hostile code on the analyst's workstation, and the draft lacked sandbox enforcement and private-address blocking. veto_lift adopted: all eight lift conditions are in this spec, and redteam_lead's hardening (WebRTC, QUIC, NTLM, DNS rebinding, request cap, no stealth plugins) is folded in. legal_dpo: retention, scrubbed HAR, redacted victims' data. cti_lead and osint_investigator: cloaking caveat and a court-usable evidence record. analyst_ux: embedded, captioned screenshots. LIGHT and explicit-only: default_enabled false. Target contact: tier n/a; Playwright Apache-2.0: terms open.

Synthesizer conditions (background; the final conditions win on conflict): Optional extra (pip playwright + chromium), LIGHT opsec, off by default: runs only through a non-corporate egress profile (egress_profiles) when the target is tagged malicious/phishing; accept_downloads=False, no form submission, no credentials, 30 s budget, stop on challenge/CAPTCHA pages (rule 1), fresh browser context per page; outputs screenshot, DOM, HAR as hashed evidence. When contact is not allowed, reuse screenshots of existing PUBLIC urlscan scans found by search (page.domain: filter; never submit a scan).
Notes: Preserves phishing evidence for takedown and triage without exposing the corporate IP.
